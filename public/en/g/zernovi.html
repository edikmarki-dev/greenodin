Cereals such as wheat, barley, buckwheat and corn are a large part of our diet. They are a source of essential carbohydrates, protein and other important nutrients. To get a rich cereal harvest and high grain quality, you need to look after the soil and plants properly. GREENODIN cereal fertilizers help with that.

Why are fertilizers so important for cereals?

Cereals need substantial resources to grow and produce a good crop. They remove many nutrients from the soil that have to be replaced. GREENODIN organo-mineral fertilizers for cereals give the plants all the macro- and micronutrients they need to stay healthy and productive.

Benefits of GREENODIN fertilizers for cereals

Nutrient balance

Our fertilizers give cereals a balanced set of nutrients, including nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and others, supporting plant growth and development and the formation of large, good-quality grain.

Better soil structure

GREENODIN fertilizers also improve soil structure, which raises its water-holding capacity and the availability of nutrients to plants. This is especially important in dry regions. Soil fertility is restored as well.

Organic origin

We are proud that our fertilizers are made from organic components, which makes them safe for the environment and nature. GREENODIN fertilizers support not only plant health but also the ecological balance.

Higher yield

Using GREENODIN fertilizers correctly can raise cereal yields considerably, which matters to farmers and consumers alike. You get a more abundant grain harvest, which supports food security.

Using GREENODIN fertilizers for cereals

Using our fertilizers is simple and effective. Follow the pack recommendations to dose correctly and apply them at the right time. This helps you get excellent results and a good-quality grain harvest.
