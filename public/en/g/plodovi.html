Every spring is not only nature reawakening but also the rewarding work of caring for your trees. Fertilizers deserve special attention here, as they are the key to a rich harvest and a beautiful orchard.

Why does feeding matter for trees?

Trees, like all living things, need nutrients to stay healthy and thrive. Feeding is not just a procedure but care for every tree, where well-chosen fertilizers are the key to balanced development, disease resistance and, of course, a rich harvest. Feeding with organic fertilizers needs to be done right.

GREENODIN stands out for its unique formula. These are granulated complex organo-mineral fertilizers with no room for chemistry but everything strong, healthy, fruitful trees need.
