The role of GREENODIN fertilizers in industrial crop development

Industrial crops have specific nutrient needs and require closer attention. That is why GREENODIN fertilizers are an integral part of growing these crops successfully.

Advantages of GREENODIN fertilizers for industrial crops

A balanced set of nutrients

Complex GREENODIN fertilizers give industrial crops the best combination of macro- and micronutrients. The balance includes nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), potassium (K) and vital micronutrients such as iron, copper, zinc and manganese.

Better soil fertility

GREENODIN fertilizers help improve soil structure and fertility. This raises the soil's ability to hold water and nutrients.

Organic origin

GREENODIN products are made from organic components. That makes them safe for the environment and nature. With GREENODIN fertilizers you can be sure you are contributing to the ecological balance.

Higher productivity

Using GREENODIN fertilizers correctly raises the yield of industrial crops. That lets both farmers and raw-material producers grow their output and improve their economics.

Resistance to stress and disease

GREENODIN helps industrial crops resist stress such as drought or bad weather, and also diseases and pests. That helps protect the harvest even in unfavorable farming conditions.

How to apply GREENODIN fertilizers to industrial crops

Using our fertilizers is simple and effective. In open ground, specialized mechanical equipment is needed to spread GREENODIN fertilizers evenly and efficiently. That ensures optimal nutrient uptake across the whole soil area. Under cover, such as in greenhouses or conservatories, it is recommended to mix GREENODIN fertilizers into the growing mix. This enriches the substrate the plants will grow in and creates optimal conditions for their growth.

On home plots, spread GREENODIN fertilizers evenly around the plants following the pack instructions. After applying GREENODIN to the soil, water it in if you can. This helps the fertilizer dissolve quickly and spreads the nutrients evenly across the whole planted area. So whatever the type of farming practice, GREENODIN fertilizers are a flexible, effective way to enrich the soil and give plants all the nutrients they need.
