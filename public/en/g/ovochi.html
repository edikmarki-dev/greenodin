Growing vegetables is not only economical and healthy but also a fascinating adventure that can bring you a bountiful harvest. However, for your vegetables to grow strong and healthy and yield well, they need nutrients. GREENODIN vegetable fertilizers, developed specifically for vegetable crops in line with Regulation (EU) 2018/848, will help with that.

Why does feeding matter for vegetables?

Vegetables develop very actively and set fruit within a relatively short time. To reach their full potential and reward you with tasty, nutritious produce, they need extra feeding. GREENODIN vegetable fertilizers are designed to enrich the soil with all the necessary trace elements and nutrients, creating ideal conditions for optimal growth and development.

GREENODIN vegetable fertilizers are a key element of successful vegetable growing. Our products enrich your soil with the components that form the basis of healthy plants and a rich harvest.

Give your garden the best conditions for vegetables with GREENODIN organic fertilizers.

Advantages of GREENODIN fertilizers for vegetables

Nutrient balance

Our fertilizers give vegetables even, balanced nutrition. Vegetables get all the substances they need, in the right amounts and the right proportions.

Organic composition

We use organic components in our fertilizers, which makes them safe for you and the environment. Organic fertilizers also improve soil structure and restore its fertility.

Resistance to diseases and pests

GREENODIN vegetable fertilizers help vegetables become less vulnerable to diseases and pests, which reduces the need for chemical treatments and supports a revival of organic farming.

Yield

With GREENODIN vegetable fertilizers your harvest will be bigger and of better quality. You get more tasty vegetables for your table and for sale.

Using GREENODIN fertilizers for vegetables

It's simple. It's simple: follow the recommendations on the pack and dose the fertilizer according to the type of vegetable and its growth stage. The right fertilizer will help you get excellent results in your garden.
