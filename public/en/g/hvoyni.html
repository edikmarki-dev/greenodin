Conifers

There are several types of fertilizer for conifers, and the best of them is GREENODIN BROWN with a pH of 5.0. It is ready to work naturally and acts on several fronts at once: it gently acidifies the soil and supplies the plants with the nutrients they need. This comes from the composition of GREENODIN BROWN granular organic conifer fertilizer, which also works as a soil conditioner and a soil restorer. GREENODIN BROWN improves soil structure, breaks down organic matter and delivers the necessary nutrients, so it can be used as an autumn conifer fertilizer as well as in spring.

Attention! Since the optimal acidity for evergreens is pH 3.5, in addition to the conifer fertilizer GREENODIN BROWN you should add acidic peat. The microorganisms in GREENODIN BROWN will then boost the fertilizer's own action and process the acidic peat, strengthening its effect and releasing the components needed.

The base rate of the conifer fertilizer GREENODIN BROWN is 25 grams per square meter broadcast, or 25 grams per running meter in a row, or 25 grams under the conifer itself. The commercial rate is 250 kg/ha. Because the product accumulates in the soil, the maximum rate of 25 g/sq. m (250 kg/ha) can be lowered to 8 g/sq. m (80 kg/ha) by the 6th season, cutting 20% each season with no loss of effectiveness, which gives a very large economic effect.

How the bacteria in the fertilizer work

Nitrogen-fixing bacteria retain nitrogen, which promotes the growth of young shoots and stimulates photosynthesis. Phosphate-solubilising bacteria supply the plant with phosphorus, which supports root development. Potassium-mobilising bacteria do the same with potassium, which helps the overall health of conifers and their resistance to stress. Because of this effect, GREENODIN BROWN is sometimes called a needle fertilizer.

Specialists also classify GREENODIN BROWN as a dedicated conifer fertilizer because of its well-balanced combination of nutrients for growing coniferous trees and shrubs. The microorganisms in GREENODIN BROWN increase the content of trace elements that are important for healthy conifer growth, which is why it is regarded as a fertilizer against needle yellowing.

Feeding conifers

Feeding is an important part of conifer care. The main nutrients conifers need are nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P) and potassium (K), plus some trace elements, which are supplied by the microorganisms in GREENODIN BROWN.

Feeding conifers in spring

Feeding conifers in spring is especially important, because this is the period of active growth and development for many conifer species. A base dose of GREENODIN BROWN will improve the soil structure, break down organic matter and supply nutrients to the conifers. Spread the organic fertilizer near the root zone and gently mix it into the soil. Alongside feeding, make sure conifers are watered regularly, especially during active spring growth.

Feeding conifers in autumn

Autumn feeding matters too: before winter, plants prepare for dormancy and store resources. The components of GREENODIN BROWN help conifers strengthen their root system, preserve energy reserves and cope better with winter. Water conifers well before the frosts so they can store enough moisture in their tissues before winter dormancy.

Growth stimulant for conifers

The organic fertilizer GREENODIN BROWN is also the best growth stimulant for conifers thanks to its humic and fulvic acids and agronomically useful microorganisms. These organic acids are a highly effective, environmentally clean biostimulant. GREENODIN BROWN contains a combination of many different molecules with similar biochemical properties. Fulvic acid raises plant metabolism and improves root development, as humic acid does, increasing nutrient uptake and retention and the ecological health of the soil. Humic acids give the plant energy to resist disease, and also strengthen the root and improve growth quality.

Caring for conifers

Good care keeps conifers looking bright and growing strongly, and that is quite achievable if you follow the basic advice below.

Basic care tips

Where to plant conifers

Choose the right spot for conifers based on their light needs: most of them require full sun or partial shade. They can also be sensitive to wind, so protect them from strong gusts.

Watering conifers

Water conifers regularly, especially in dry spells. Keep in mind that different species may have different watering needs. Keep the root zone sufficiently moist and avoid both drying out and waterlogging.

Soil for conifers

Conifers grow best in well-drained soil rich in organic matter. Make sure the soil drains well and the roots have free access to air.

Mulching conifers

Mulching under conifers in autumn helps retain moisture, keep the soil temperature stable and protect the roots from freezing. Use organic mulch such as dry leaves, straw or bark and spread it around the base of the plants, ideally with a base dose of GREENODIN BROWN so that it breaks down better.

Pruning conifers

Prune conifers to maintain their shape, size and health. Remove dead, diseased or damaged branches to improve air circulation and keep growth healthy. Small dry twigs, bark and needles are a useful addition to mulch.

Lawn

A well-kept, lush lawn is the goal of every garden or country-house owner. But getting there takes proper care and attention.

A lawn is an area of land with a grass cover created by people.

Lawn care: the essentials

Lawn care is an important part of keeping a beautiful and environmentally clean yard. Proper lawn care combines regular maintenance with the right choice of fertilizer.

With the unique eco-friendly GREENODIN fertilizer, lawn care takes a minimum of water, time and money.

To keep your lawn in the best condition, we recommend these important steps:

  1. Watch the soil acidityThis is a very important environmental factor. Test the soil before planting and check the pH level from time to time during growing, so that it stays in the optimal range.
    • pH test kits: the simplest and most accessible method. They come with instructions and give a result in a few minutes.
    • Soil pH meter: a more accurate method; an electronic device measures soil electrical conductivity, which is related to pH. It costs more but gives more accurate results.
  2. Clear the lawnRemove leaves and debris with a leaf rake.
  3. Aerate the soilThe soil needs oxygen. Using the recommended fertilizer, however, means you need not worry about this: it creates the best conditions on its own.
  4. Water in the eveningWhen the heat has eased. This can save up to 40% of water.
  5. Mow regularlyIn dry weather, mow the lawn regularly to keep it at the length you want.
  6. Let the grass rest in winterDo no treatments in the winter months, until the end of March or the start of April.
  7. Fertilize on time, with the right productsFor the best health and appearance of the lawn, it is important to apply fertilizer at the right time.

With GREENODIN organo-mineral granular fertilizers, the lawn only needs feeding twice a year: in spring and in autumn. These fertilizers are made without chemicals or GMOs, so they are safe for people, bees and animals. You can feed your lawn without worrying about your pets' health.

Fertilizing the lawn with GREENODIN products

With so many fertilizers on the market, it can be hard to tell which suits your lawn best. The good news is that choosing an organic fertilizer is a simple, effective way to improve the health and beauty of your lawn while looking after environmental safety. Mineral fertilizers, also known as chemical fertilizers, deliver a quick rush of nutrients to the soil. But they can upset the fragile balance of the soil ecosystem, which over the long term may lead to soil degradation and poorer plant health. In addition, mineral fertilizers reduce the nutrient content of the lawn and may release harmful chemicals into the environment. Pets and other animals take a dose of harmful substances along with such grass. Bees and insects even more so. Organic fertilizers, on the other hand, improve the overall condition of the soil and your lawn. They are made from natural materials. With organic fertilizers you not only give your lawn the nutrients it needs to grow, but also improve the soil structure and fertility for later use.
