Legumes such as peas, beans, kidney beans and chickpeas are a valuable source of protein and other nutrients in our diet. For healthy growth and a rich harvest, they need the right fertilizers. GREENODIN legume fertilizers come to help in this important work.

Why do pulses need fertilizer?

Pulses actively take up nitrogen from the soil for growth and development. They can also absorb nitrogen from the air through a symbiotic relationship with root bacteria. However, higher yields and quality require additional fertilization, especially on poor soils. GREENODIN fertilizers give pulse crops all the elements they need to grow and develop successfully.

Benefits of GREENODIN fertilizers for legumes

Nutrient balance

Our fertilizers give legumes a balanced set of nutrients, including nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and others, supporting healthy growth and quality pods.

Support for nitrogen fixation

GREENODIN supports more effective nitrogen fixation in legume roots, which improves soil quality and reduces the need for chemical fertilizers.

Organic origin

Our fertilizers are made from organic components, which makes them environmentally safe and helps protect nature.

Higher yields

Using GREENODIN fertilizers correctly gives a more abundant legume harvest, which matters to producers and buyers alike.

Using GREENODIN fertilizers for pulses

Using our fertilizers is simple and effective. Spread them around the legume plants following the pack instructions and water. The fertilizers start releasing useful elements, supporting growth and development. In open ground, spreading GREENODIN calls for special mechanical equipment to get an even and efficient spread over the whole soil.

After GREENODIN is applied, watering the soil is recommended (if possible). This helps the fertilizer dissolve quickly and spreads its nutrients evenly across the sown area. It matters so that plants get the nutrients they need for healthy growth and development. Under cover, such as greenhouses or conservatories, it is recommended to mix GREENODIN into the growing mix. This enriches the substrate the plants will grow in and gives them optimal conditions.

So whatever the type of farming, GREENODIN offers a flexible, effective way to enrich the soil and give plants all the nutrients they need. By following the application advice and using suitable equipment, you can get the most out of GREENODIN fertilizers and achieve excellent results in your farming.
