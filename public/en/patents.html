Trial reports

Research report · 2018

Effect of organo-mineral fertilizers on winter wheat productivity in the southern Steppe of Ukraine

Mykolaiv State Agricultural Experimental Station of the Institute of Irrigated Agriculture of the NAAS

Yields on the fertilized plots were on average 26.9% (0.63 t/ha) higher than the control. GREENODIN GRAY at 150 or 300 kg/ha before pre-sowing cultivation is recommended: 3.99–4.29 t/ha with 128–140% profitability.

Research report · 2019

Effect of different rates of organo-mineral fertilizers on sunflower development and yield

Mykolaiv State Agricultural Experimental Station of the Institute of Irrigated Agriculture of the NAAS

The highest yields of the Oplot hybrid, 3.09 and 3.07 t/ha, were obtained with 300 and 500 kg/ha of GREENODIN GRAY, which is 0.04–1.17 t/ha more than the other treatments. Profitability at 300 kg/ha was 154%.

Research report · 2019

Effectiveness of organo-mineral fertilizers on yield and the possibility of obtaining environmentally clean strawberries

Mykolaiv State Agricultural Experimental Station of the Institute of Irrigated Agriculture of the NAAS

The maximum yield of strawberry variety Rozana Kyivska was 66.2 c/ha with GREENODIN GRAY at 500 kg/ha broadcast and N45P30K15 fertigation. GREENODIN GRAY reduced the copper and zinc content of the berries by 23–30%.

Research report · 2020

Effect of organo-mineral fertilizers on winter garlic development and yield

Mykolaiv State Agricultural Experimental Station of the Institute of Irrigated Agriculture of the NAAS

The highest winter garlic yield, 15.3–15.9 t/ha, came from GREENODIN GRAY or GREENODIN MICROBOX at 300 kg/ha before primary tillage. Profitability was 139–140%.

Research report · 2022

Effect of GREENODIN organic fertilizer on potato, soybean and maize yields

Institute of Feed Research and Agriculture of Podillia of the NAAS, Vinnytsia

GREENODIN GRAY at 250 kg/ha increased yields compared with the control: grain maize +17%, potatoes +14%, soybeans +12%.

Research report · 2016

Field trials of GREENODIN soil restorers

State Institution "Southern Scientific and Technical Center for Testing and Introduction of New Machinery and Technologies", Odesa

GREENODIN soil restorers increased the yield of Zoria Ukrainy wheat: BLACK +25%, GRAY +30% grain. Application with spreaders followed by harrowing, or before discing, is recommended.
