GREENODIN™ organic fertilizers for orchids contain all the micronutrients needed for healthy plant growth and development. They help orchids build roots and promote flowering. GREENODIN™ fertilizers are made from natural ingredients and contain no chemicals harmful to plants or the environment.

Orchid care. How to care for orchids: a few important points to keep in mind.

Watering: orchids like humidity and should be watered often enough for the substrate to stay moist but not wet. One property of GREENODIN fertilizers is to retain moisture in the soil and keep it from evaporating, which makes the orchid less demanding. It is best to water from below, avoiding water on the leaves.

Light: orchids love light but should not be exposed to direct sunlight. The best place for them is one with soft, indirect light.

Temperature: orchids love warmth and should be grown at 18 to 25 degrees. Using GREENODIN BROWN increases the flower's stress resistance and helps it cope with temperature swings. Even so, ideal conditions should still be maintained.

Fertilizer: orchids need regular feeding with a special fertilizer for flowering plants. It is important to use fertilizers with the right balance of micronutrients. If your substrate differs from the recommended one and you are not yet planning to repot the orchid, feed it by adding GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer about once every six months.

These are the basic recommendations for orchid care. But there are some additional tips that can help your plants grow and bloom even better:

  • Repotting: orchids need repotting every 2-3 years, when the roots fill the whole pot

Diseases and pests: orchids can be hit by diseases and pests, so check them regularly for webbing, spots or yellow leaves. With GREENODIN organic fertilizers you avoid such problems, because beneficial bacteria suppress the pathogenic flora of the soil.

Flowering regime: for an orchid to bloom, you need to keep to the right light and temperature regime and feed it regularly.

How to repot an orchid

Repotting an orchid is important for its healthy growth and flowering. Here are the main steps:

  • Choose the right time: it is best to repot an orchid between blooms so as not to damage flowers and leaves
  • Carefully lift the plant out of the pot, trying not to damage the roots
  • Clear the roots of rotten or dried soil and of dead roots. If the roots are too tangled, trim them slightly to make room for new growth
  • Plant it in the new pot: place the plant in the new pot and fill it with the prepared soil. Make sure the plant sits at the same level as before
  • Watering: after repotting, water the orchid and give it time to adapt to its new conditions in a cool place

It is important to remember that in ordinary substrate orchids do not like excess moisture in the pot, so water carefully and allow enough time to dry between waterings. Although GREENODIN organic fertilizer retains moisture, it is stored in a special silica matrix. Water is drawn up by capillary action only in the amounts needed, which protects against excess moisture in the pot.

Soil for orchids

Preparing the growing medium for orchids is important to give them the conditions they need for growth and flowering:

  • Orchid soil must drain well to avoid standing moisture, which can lead to root rot
  • Orchids like neutral or slightly acidic soil with a pH of around 6.0-6.5. That means the soil should be mildly acidic, but not too much so
How to determine soil acidity

If you do not know the acidity of your soil, be sure to check it! Every plant needs its own acidity, and if you ignore acidity requirements, diseases will not leave your plant alone. There are several ways to test soil to determine its pH level:

  • pH test kits: the simplest and most affordable method. They can be bought in garden-equipment shops or online. They usually come with instructions and give a result within a few minutes
  • Soil pH meter: a more accurate way of measuring pH, using an electronic device to measure the soil's electrical conductivity, which is related to pH. It can cost more but gives more precise results

Test the soil before planting and then check the pH regularly while the plant grows to make sure it stays in the optimal range.

Common orchid diseases

The special fertilizer for orchids from GREENODIN is designed not just to protect against diseases but to prevent them from appearing. This is achieved by boosting the plant's “immunity” and using protective beneficial bacteria that suppress the pathogenic flora of the soil

Without such protection, orchids can be hit by various diseases, but the most common are:

  • Powdery mildew: a fungus that causes white or grey spots on leaves and stems
  • Leaf spot: another fungus that causes yellow or brown spots on leaves
  • Black leg: a bacterial disease that causes rotting of the roots and root system
  • Black spot: another bacterial disease that causes black spots on leaves and stems
  • Brown root rot. Causes: high substrate moisture, poor soil quality, or alkalinisation of the soil from watering with hard water
  • If your flower grows in a favourable environment and receives proper natural nutrition without chemicals, it will not be prone to these diseases!

GREENODIN complex organic fertilizer creates exactly such favourable conditions. The presence of all the necessary micronutrients supports the plant's immunity, and the beneficial bacteria create the right environment in the soil and protect against harmful fungi and the like.

Which organic fertilizer is best for orchids?

GREENODIN BROWN fertilizer for orchids: 25 g per 1 litre of substrate when repotting. The granules release nutrition gradually and maintain the acidic reaction of the substrate.

How much fertilizer do orchids need per pot or per 1 litre of substrate?

25 g per 1 L of substrate when planting or repotting. Mix the granules into the potting mix. The following year, reduce the rate by 20%.

When should fertilizer be applied to orchids?

When planting or repotting: mix the granules into the substrate. After that, the slow-release action keeps the plant fed.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Measure out the right amount of granules, mix them evenly into the potting mix, plant and water.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for orchids?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
