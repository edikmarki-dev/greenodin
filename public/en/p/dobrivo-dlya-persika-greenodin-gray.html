GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for peach is an innovative complex organic granular fertilizer and soil conditioner based on leonardite and silica-bearing natural minerals, with a medium organic content and degree of mineralisation. GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral complex granular fertilizers for peach are developed in line with international environmental requirements, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

Successful peach growing requires a comprehensive approach to tree care, including proper fertilization. GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral fertilizer for peach of the “Organic Standard” class is the key to healthy growth and a high peach yield of up to 50 kg per tree. Creating favourable conditions for peach development and fruiting starts with providing ideal soil.

Caring for peaches with GREENODIN GRAY fertilizers

Peaches prefer well-drained, nutrient-rich soil with an acidity of pH 6.7–7.0. Thanks to its composition, GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for peach improves soil structure, enriching it with the necessary nutrients and creating optimal conditions for peach growth. The fertilizer has an ideal acidity of pH 7.0, which makes it possible to adapt even unfavourable soil to optimal conditions.

Dosage and application timing

The dosage and timing of GREENODIN GRAY complex organo-mineral fertilizer for peach depend on the age of the tree, the condition of the soil and the plant's development stage. In general, the application rate ranges from 80 to 250 kg/ha, or from 800 g to 2.5 kg per 100 m², or 8–25 g/m².

The cumulative effect of the product allows the maximum rate to be reduced from 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) to 80 kg/ha (8 g/m²), cutting it by 20% every season.

This granular complex organo-mineral fertilizer suits all soil types, including depleted and contaminated ones.

It has a medium moisture-holding capacity, saving and structuring irrigation water.

Feeding peach

Every year, as the tree comes out of dormancy, peach is fed in spring, and when the tree is being prepared for winter, feeding in autumn is a must.

Feeding peach should be combined with watering: 3 times per season for early varieties and 6 for late ones. One watering is 15 to 50 litres per tree, or 2–7 buckets, wetting the soil at most to the depth of the roots, up to 70 cm.

Water when the sun is not active: early in the morning or in the evening. Peach is first watered during the spring feeding in March, before the buds swell. The next watering is done with the summer feeding in June, towards the end of the month. Summer feeding of peach ends in the first half of August. The final feeding is done after fruiting, right after harvest and crown formation.

Components of GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for peach

Organic content, 30%: leonardite and peat enriched with humic, fulvic and amino acids stimulate growth and give uniform, confident germination.

The biological component, a consortium of agronomically beneficial microorganisms (including Azotobacter, Bacillus and Paenibacillus), stimulates root system development, increases nutrient availability and speeds up the mineralisation of residual nutrients.

Mineral component: enriched with silicon, calcium, magnesium, sulphur, iron, nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, providing nutritional support for healthy growth and development of peaches.

Benefits of using GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for peach. Higher soil fertility and better agronomic characteristics, which strengthens the peach roots and the plant as a whole.

Stimulated plant growth and yield up to 65% higher.

Improved vitality of peaches and their resistance to disease.

A 30% saving in irrigation water and its structuring.

Reduced use of agrochemicals and a transition to organic farming. The organic nature of the crop shows in the large quantity and high quality of the peaches, which meet top-grade standards.

One of the key advantages of using GREENODIN GRAY complex organo-mineral fertilizer for peach is a significant improvement in the fruit's organoleptic properties. Thanks to balanced nutrition, peaches gain a more pronounced taste and aroma and become especially juicy and sweet.

Using GREENODIN GRAY helps form fruit that stands out not only for high yields but also for distinct organoleptic qualities, making each fruit a true embodiment of the ideal sweet peach with a rich aroma. Together with Organic Standard, this result makes peaches especially attractive to consumers and profitable for farmers seeking premium-grade produce.

Using GREENODIN GRAY fertilizers for peach not only helps produce a rich harvest of juicy, sweet peaches but is also a step towards sustainable agriculture, reducing dependence on chemical fertilizers and supporting the health of the soil and the environment.

Which organic fertilizer is best for peach?

GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for peach. Apply in the tree-trunk circle twice a year; the silica component helps retain moisture and the beneficial bacteria feed the roots.

How much fertilizer does peach need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to peach?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for peach?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
