GREENODIN MICROBOX substrate

GREENODIN MICROBOX complex organic fertilizer, substrate, is an innovative organo-mineral mixture based on silicon-containing natural minerals and peat for protected farming in greenhouses and glasshouses, for all soil types

Purpose

Speeds up plant growth

Creates a local ecosystem with agronomically beneficial microorganisms: Bacillus subtilis, Azotobacter, Paenibacillus polymyxa, Enterococcus, Lactobacillus

A base fertilizer for farms and large areas. The line is chosen based on a soil analysis.

the soil substrate is used for open and protected (greenhouse, glasshouse) farming on soils with a low content of mineral nutrition elements; a universal solution for seedlings and vegetable, flower, fruit and berry crops; a balanced composition for all types of plants and soil

  • peat, leonardite, diatomite
  • modified silicon-containing minerals. Active substance: active silicon.
  • macro- and microelements needed for plant growth and for greater resistance to stress factors; agronomically beneficial microflora

What is GREENODIN MICROBOX used for?

GREENODIN MICROBOX is a soil substrate based on peat, leonardite and diatomite with beneficial microorganisms. For seedlings and vegetable, flower, fruit and berry crops in open ground and under cover.

What is the application rate of GREENODIN MICROBOX per hectare?

The base rate is 250 kg/ha (25 g/m², 2.5 kg per 100 m²). Thanks to the cumulative effect the rate is cut by 20% every year, down to 80 kg/ha from the 5th–6th season. The exact rate depends on the soil analysis.

How do you apply GREENODIN MICROBOX in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are applied with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader before primary tillage or before sowing, after which the field is harrowed or disked.

What packaging does GREENODIN MICROBOX come in?

Polyethylene bags of 5 kg, polypropylene sacks of 25 and 50 kg. For farms and wholesale buyers, big bags of 600 to 1,100 kg.

Is there scientific evidence of effectiveness?

Yes, the fertilizer has been tested by research institutions of NAAS. 2020: the highest winter garlic yield, 15.3–15.9 t/ha, came from GREENODIN GRAY or GREENODIN MICROBOX at 300 kg/ha before primary tillage. Profitability was 139–140%. The reports are on the Documents tab.

Can different GREENODIN lines be combined?

Yes. Fertilizers from different GREENODIN lines can be combined depending on the acidity and composition of the soil. GREENODIN also lets you gradually reduce mineral fertilizers, up to giving them up completely.

Can it be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and animals?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.
