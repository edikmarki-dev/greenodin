GREENODIN MELIOR

GREENODIN MELIOR organic fertilizer is an innovative complex granulated organo-mineral soil improver, reclamation agent and ameliorant of the Organic Standard class for effective soil deacidification.

Purpose

Restoring and increasing fertility by deacidifying soils

Better uptake of essential elements, above all nitrogen, whose use can be cut by up to 30% without yield loss

A base fertilizer for farms and large areas. The line is chosen based on a soil analysis.

  • A natural material with a high calcium carbonate content, with added silicas and further enriched with natural polysilicic acids and agronomically beneficial microorganisms. It provides a high content of the microelements needed for plant growth and greater resistance to stress factors.

Technical specifications: TU U 20.1-13856524-001:2014. Guaranteed shelf life of the packaged mixture is three years from the date of manufacture. Storage conditions: during transport, loading and unloading the product must be protected from precipitation. Store on sites protected from precipitation, groundwater and direct sunlight. GREENODIN MELIOR was developed by leading Ukrainian scientists. The product is certified under the Standard for the production of inputs permitted in organic agriculture and processing that comply with the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2018/848, certificate No. 23-0504-01/02 dated 16.10.2023. The manufacturer reserves the right to change the packaging design

It depends on the agrochemical characteristics of the soil, but on average: for open and protected farming (greenhouses, glasshouses) 400 kg of GREENODIN organic fertilizers per 1 ha or 4 kg of GREENODIN per 100 m² of land, spread

when growing seedlings, ornamental plants, flowers: 0.4 l of GREENODIN organic fertilizers per 1 m³ of soil mix, mix in, or 0.4 kg of GREENODIN organic fertilizers per 1 m³ of soil mix, mix in, or 2 teaspoons of GREENODIN organic fertilizers per 1 l of soil mix, mix in

The cumulative effect of the product lets you lower the maximum rate from 400 kg/ha (40 g/sq. m) to 100 kg/ha (10 g/sq. m) by the 5th season of use

It is recommended to mix the substrate into the soil mix for protected farming and use in pots, and in open-field farming

  • spread the fertilizer by hand or with mechanical equipment, followed by watering.

GREENODIN organo-mineral mixtures are supplied in polyethylene bags of 5 kg and polypropylene sacks of 50 kg. The polypropylene is white and strong, 65–70 µm thick.

For wholesale buyers, GREENODIN eco mixtures are supplied in big bags of 600 kg, 1000 kg and 1100 kg.

Stimulated plant development and up to 30% faster crop growth, with irrigation water use cut by up to 40%

After the guaranteed storage period expires, the fertilizers can be used as intended once the quality indicators have been checked against the technical specifications.

What is GREENODIN MELIOR used for?

GREENODIN MELIOR is an ameliorant based on natural calcium carbonate and silicas for deacidifying acidic and depleted soils and restoring their fertility.

What is the application rate of GREENODIN MELIOR per hectare?

The base rate is 250 kg/ha (25 g/m², 2.5 kg per 100 m²). Thanks to the cumulative effect the rate is cut by 20% every year, down to 80 kg/ha from the 5th–6th season. The exact rate depends on the soil analysis.

How do you apply GREENODIN MELIOR in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are applied with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader before primary tillage or before sowing, after which the field is harrowed or disked.

What packaging does GREENODIN MELIOR come in?

Polyethylene bags of 5 kg, polypropylene sacks of 25 and 50 kg. For farms and wholesale buyers, big bags of 600 to 1,100 kg.

Can different GREENODIN lines be combined?

Yes. Fertilizers from different GREENODIN lines can be combined depending on the acidity and composition of the soil. GREENODIN also lets you gradually reduce mineral fertilizers, up to giving them up completely.

Can it be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and animals?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.
