GREENODIN BROWN is the best fertilizer for thuja, as it is an outstanding organo-mineral product containing active silicon, humic and fulvic acids, organic matter and beneficial microorganisms that support healthy plant growth and development. GREENODIN BROWN is effective at preventing and relieving stress and works well for thuja in any season and weather. That makes it an excellent thuja fertilizer in both spring and autumn, supplying the plants with the nutrients they need, including macro- and micronutrients.

The active silicon in GREENODIN BROWN strengthens the roots of thuja and the plants as a whole, making them more resistant to harsh weather and damage. Regular use of GREENODIN BROWN promotes healthy, even growth and improves the appearance of thuja. The questions “What should I feed thuja?” and “How do I feed thuja in spring?” are answered by regular use of GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer. The recommended rate is 25 g per square metre, or per litre of substrate for container-grown plants. At commercial scale, it is 250 kg per hectare.

Soil for thuja

Thuja does best in a dedicated soil that provides optimal growing conditions. It should drain well, hold moisture, be slightly acidic at pH 5.5 and contain the necessary nutrients. GREENODIN BROWN effectively acidifies the base soil and enriches it with nutrients.

Since too much moisture can lead to root rot, add drainage material such as perlite or sand to the soil.

This approach is especially convenient and effective for growing thuja in pots or containers, as the mix provides good drainage and nutrition. Remember that GREENODIN BROWN improves soil structure, increases its water-holding capacity and makes nutrients more available to plants. It also supports beneficial soil microorganisms, which has a positive effect on soil fertility.

Where to plant thuja. Like other conifers, thuja grows best in sun or partial shade, on open sites with good air circulation. It does best with 6–8 hours of sunlight a day but can also grow in partial shade, although much more slowly.

Wind protection. Thuja is fairly wind-resistant, but strong winds can damage its brittle branches, so choose a site sheltered from strong winds. GREENODIN BROWN significantly strengthens the plant and makes it more wind-resistant.

Also consider the size of mature thuja and leave enough space between plants for them to grow freely. Usually 1 to 3 metres between saplings is recommended so that each plant gets enough light, air and soil nutrients.

To create a dense hedge or green wall, plant thuja closer together, about 1 metre apart. Over time the plants will form a solid green screen.

The optimal spacing depends on the thuja species you are growing and the conditions on your site, and may vary with species and regional climate.

Planting thuja

Proper planting sets thuja up for healthy growth. Once you have prepared the soil and chosen the right spot, all that remains is to prepare the plant, plant or transplant it and give it proper care for the most beautiful thuja nature can offer.

Preparing thuja for planting or transplanting

If the sapling was grown in a container, gently remove it and loosen the root ball to encourage root growth. If the thuja is bare-root, soak the roots in water for a few minutes before planting.

Planting or transplanting thuja

Dig the hole in advance, twice the size of the root system or, better still, of the crown. Place the thuja in the centre so the top of the root collar sits slightly below soil level. Fill the hole with enriched soil mixed with GREENODIN BROWN, firming it around the roots. Make sure the plant stands straight and stable.

Water the plant thoroughly after planting to give it initial moisture. Keep the soil moist during the first year, especially in dry spells. GREENODIN BROWN retains moisture and makes up for late watering thanks to its water-holding capacity, saving 20–30% of irrigation water. This makes GREENODIN BROWN, an organic soil restorer and conditioner, the ideal fertilizer for planting thuja.

Watering thuja

Water regularly during the first year, especially in droughts. Thuja needs moderate watering to keep the soil moist but not waterlogged. How often depends on conditions such as weather, soil type and plant age. In general, water once or twice a week when there is no rain or it is hot, making sure the water reaches the root zone. You can water at the base of the plant or use drip irrigation.

When watering at the base, avoid wetting the foliage and trunk to prevent fungal disease. Check the topsoil: if it is dry, it is time to water. If the soil starts cracking from drought, water heavily straight away. Avoid overwatering, and remember that thuja prefers good drainage and does not tolerate flooding or waterlogging. GREENODIN BROWN, like other GREENODIN fertilizers, retains moisture and prevents the soil from cracking as it dries, saving 20–30% of irrigation water.

Controlling weeds around thuja

Weed control around thuja is an important part of care that gives the plant good growing conditions. Pull weeds by hand from around the thuja, taking care not to damage its roots. Mow grass and weeds around the plant regularly to stop them spreading, especially before they flower and set seed. Chopped clippings make useful mulch.

Mulching thuja

Spread a 5–7 cm layer of mulch around the base of the plant, leaving a small gap around the trunk so it is not smothered. Mulch retains moisture, reduces competition from weeds and protects roots from temperature swings. The best mulch is made from chopped straw, dry grass (including weeds), small thuja twigs and pine shavings with GREENODIN BROWN added. Mulch in spring or autumn, once the soil has warmed up or cooled down. Check the mulch from time to time and top it up as needed.

Feeding thuja

For optimal growth, feed thuja regularly with GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer. In spring, 25 g under each tree, watered in, is enough. GREENODIN BROWN enriches the soil at planting and the mulch when you prepare it. The base rate is 25 g per square metre, or per litre of substrate for container-grown plants, and 250 kg per hectare commercially.

Thanks to the cumulative action of every type of GREENODIN, the maximum rate can be reduced from 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) to 80 kg/ha (8 g/m²) by the 6th season by cutting it by 20% every year with no loss of effectiveness, which brings significant savings.

Thuja 'Smaragd' (Thuja occidentalis 'Smaragd') is a popular variety known for its pyramidal shape and rich green foliage. Follow the care, feeding, planting and transplanting advice above and you will have a superb, healthy and beautiful plant.

Which organic fertilizer is best for conifers?

GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer for conifers, including thuja. 25 g per 1 m² over the area, 25 g per running metre of hedge or per plant; it acidifies the soil.

How much conifer fertilizer do I need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m² over the area (2.5 kg per 100 m²), 25 g per running metre of hedge, or 25 g per plant. The commercial rate is 250 kg/ha. Reduce the rate by 20% each year.

When should I apply conifer fertilizer?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for conifers?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
