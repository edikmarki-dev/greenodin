Beetroot, also known as red beet or simply beet, is a popular root vegetable known for its sweet taste and bright colour. Successful beetroot growing is assured by using GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer, which supplies the necessary nutrients without harming the beet itself, the environment or the gardener.

Growing beetroot

To grow beetroot successfully with GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral fertilizers, take the following steps.

Soil for beetroot

Add organic matter such as compost to the soil to improve its texture and fertility. Make sure the soil is well drained and has an acidity of up to pH 7.0.

Choosing a fertilizer for beet

The ideal complex solution for applying organic fertilizers directly under beet is GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer, the application of which depends on soil parameters, including acidity, structure and organic matter content, but the maximum application rate is 25 g/m² or 250 kg/ha. The cumulative nature of GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for beetroot on any soil type allows the maximum rate of 25 g/m² (250 kg/ha) to be reduced to 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) by the 6th season of use, thanks to a 20% reduction each season, without any loss of yield, which gives a huge economic effect.

Sowing beetroot

Sow beet seeds directly into the soil about half an inch deep and 2 inches apart. Once seedlings appear, thin them to ensure correct spacing. Such enrichment ensures uniform emergence and steady germination of beetroot seed.

Watering beet

Keep the soil consistently moist but avoid overwatering, as this can cause the roots to rot. Regular watering is essential for healthy growth.

To deter pests, further improve the flavour of the root and disinfect the soil, it is advisable to water beet with salt water, at most 3 times during the whole season.

First, when the beet has put out 5 leaves; second, when the top of the root appears above the soil; and third, a week before harvest. Time for watering with the salt solution: early in the morning or in the evening after sunset, or during the day if it is overcast, making sure the beets are not in direct sunlight during watering with the salt solution. Composition and preparation of the solution: dissolve 1 tablespoon of table salt (cooking salt) in 1 litre of hot water. Iodised salt must not be used. Dilute the prepared solution in a bucket of cool water.

If the base soil for growing beet is acidic, it is advisable to add 0.5 litres of wood ash and mix thoroughly. Treat the plants with the resulting solution directly on the leaf.

Caring for beet

GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for beetroot strengthens the beetroot's natural defence against pests and diseases. At the same time, watch the plants for pests and remove them if found.

Be sure to remove weeds that sprout next to the beet.

Harvesting

Harvesting beet depending on the variety:

  • Early beet varieties usually ripen 2–3 months after planting. Harvesting can start in July or early August.
  • Mid-season varieties ripen about 3–4 months after planting. The crop can be harvested in late August or early September.
  • Late varieties ripen in 4–5 months after planting. Harvesting is done in late September or mid-October.
How do you tell when beet is ready to harvest?

Beet is ready to harvest when the roots have reached the size desired for the variety and have developed a bright colour and firm skin. The beet leaves may also begin to yellow and fall, which is another sign it is ready to harvest.

When harvesting it is advisable to use a fork, digging the roots up carefully so as not to damage them. After harvest, cut off the remaining leaves, leaving about 1–2 cm of stem, and brush the soil off the roots. Let the roots dry before storage to avoid rotting. Beet is best stored in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place. The silicon from GREENODIN GRAY absorbed by the beet ensures long storage and enhances its flavour.

Conclusion

Using GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for beetroot as an organic fertilizer significantly increases the success of growing beetroot.

It supplies the plant with the necessary nutrients, improves soil health and supports sustainable farming methods.

By following the recommended methods and dosages, growers can enjoy an abundant harvest of tasty, high-quality beetroot.

Which organic fertilizer is best for beetroot?

GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for beetroot. Applied before sowing when preparing the bed, it supplies the plants with macro- and micronutrients throughout the season.

How much fertilizer does beetroot need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to beetroot?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for beetroot?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
