GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for pepper is an innovative complex organic granular fertilizer, soil conditioner and restorer based on leonardite and silica-bearing natural minerals, with a medium organic content and degree of mineralisation. GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for pepper is completely safe for people, bees and animals.

GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral complex granular fertilizers for pepper are developed in line with international environmental requirements, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

Benefits of GREENODIN GRAY complex organic granular fertilizers for pepper

Using GREENODIN GRAY organic granular fertilizer for growing pepper, both outdoors and in greenhouses, is an effective and environmentally safe approach.

GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for pepper contains silica-bearing natural minerals and microorganisms that improve soil structure and help raise fertility. As a result, pepper roots develop better and penetrate the soil, which improves the nutrient composition and plant development.

In addition, GREENODIN GRAY for pepper has a medium moisture-holding capacity, which saves up to 30% of irrigation water. This is especially important when growing pepper in a dry climate, where every drop of water counts.

One of the main features of GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for pepper is its ability to increase plants' resistance to abiotic and biotic stress factors. The fertilizer also reduces soil toxicity and slows the development of phytopathogenic microorganisms. This is especially important for pepper, which often suffers from diseases and pests.

GREENODIN GRAY also contains the macro- and micronutrients needed for plant growth and higher yields. As a result of using this fertilizer, the quality and quantity of the pepper crop improve significantly. In sum, using GREENODIN GRAY complex granular organic fertilizer for pepper is an effective, safe and environmentally clean approach to agriculture. The video shows the difference when growing pepper with this fertilizer, using the hot pepper “Vohnyk” as an example.

However, this fertilizer is equally suitable for hot and sweet peppers: Jalapeño, Serrano, Habanero, bell pepper (red, yellow, orange, green), Capsicum (Pimiento), Italian pepper (Corno di Toro) and so on. Below are the benefits in more detail that you get from using GREENODIN GRAY complex granular organic fertilizer for pepper

Which organic fertilizer is best for pepper?

GREENODIN GRAY organic granular fertilizer for sweet pepper. Apply when preparing the soil or planting transplants; the action is prolonged, so the rate is reduced by 20% every year.

How much fertilizer does pepper need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to pepper?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for pepper?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
