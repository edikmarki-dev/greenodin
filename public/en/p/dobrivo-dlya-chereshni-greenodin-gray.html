GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for sweet cherries is an innovative organic granular fertilizer, soil conditioner and soil restorer based on leonardite and natural silica-bearing minerals, with a medium content of organic matter and a medium degree of mineralisation. GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral fertilizers for sweet cherries are developed to international environmental standards, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

A generous, tasty, high-quality harvest requires a well-planned approach to fertilizing. The all-purpose GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer ensures successful sweet cherry growing, looking after both the size and quality of the crop and providing effective nutrition that keeps the tree healthy.

Sweet cherries prefer well-drained, fertile soil with a neutral or slightly alkaline pH. Check the soil pH before fertilizing and adjust it if needed with GREENODIN GRAY, which has a neutral pH of 7.0, within the optimal range for sweet cherries (pH 6.5–7.2).

Benefits of GREENODIN GRAY organic granular fertilizer for sweet cherries

GREENODIN GRAY organic granular fertilizer is an excellent choice for sweet cherries. Its combination of leonardite with modified natural silica-bearing minerals and macro- and micronutrients gives the trees the nutrients they need and makes them more resistant to stress.

This fertilizer also has a medium water-holding capacity, saving up to 30% of irrigation water. GREENODIN GRAY also reduces soil toxicity and slows the development of plant pathogens. This means the trees take up fewer harmful substances from the soil and stay healthier all season.

In addition, GREENODIN GRAY soil restorer contains beneficial microorganisms that improve the physical properties of acidic and toxic soils. This gives the trees better nutrient uptake, growth and development.

Feeding sweet cherry trees

A healthy, generous sweet cherry crop depends on feeding the trees correctly throughout the year. GREENODIN GRAY is an effective solution for every season.

Feeding sweet cherries in early spring

Before 15 April, while the young leaves are just emerging from the buds, apply GREENODIN GRAY. It helps the tree recover after winter, supports growth for the rest of the season and strengthens it against pests and diseases.

Feeding sweet cherries in summer

After harvest, replenish the energy the tree spent on the crop: right after picking, sweet cherries start setting up the next harvest. Remember to cut out damaged and diseased branches. After deep loosening, apply GREENODIN GRAY and water the soil thoroughly. Spring and summer feeding easily ensure a large, high-quality crop this season and the next.

Feeding sweet cherries in autumn

In late September and early October, sweet cherries enter their pre-winter phase. Feed fruit trees with GREENODIN at the usual rates to strengthen them before the cold while also looking after soil fertility and structure. Only in such soil does soil life thrive and supply plants with wholesome nutrition.

Sweet cherry care

Pruning, watering and protection from pests and diseases also matter for cherry care and can noticeably raise yield, and regular, correct use of GREENODIN organic fertilizers will help you grow healthy, fruitful trees.

NOTE: pay attention to the planting site for cherry, because its roots penetrate deep into the soil. Avoid sites where the water table is less than 2 metres below the surface, and avoid acidic soils below pH 6.0, where the activity of beneficial microorganisms may be limited. Application rates of the complex organo-mineral fertilizer for cherry GREENODIN GRAY

The rate is 30 g per 1 m², lightly worked in and watered if the soil is dry. Apply from 10 cm away from the trunk out to the edge of the canopy.

It is impossible to overdose GREENODIN, as it is a completely safe organic fertilizer. The product is designed to be self-regulating, partly by building a healthy soil ecosystem. GREENODIN organic fertilizer ensures a healthy, generous crop of beautiful sweet cherries and, as a result, a healthy and happy gardener.

Which organic fertilizer is best for sweet cherries?

GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for sweet cherry trees. It improves soil structure in the tree circle and keeps the tree fed through the season thanks to its slow-release action.

How much sweet cherry fertilizer do I need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should I apply sweet cherry fertilizer?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for sweet cherries?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
