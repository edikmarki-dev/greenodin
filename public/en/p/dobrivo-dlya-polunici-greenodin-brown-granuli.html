Strawberry stays productive only briefly in one place, so the harvest depends on the soil even before planting. Structure, moisture and a clean bed matter more than the number of feedings.

Site and soil

It needs a sunny plot with a pH of 5.5–6.5. Good predecessors are onion, garlic and legumes. Do not plant strawberry after potato, tomato or eggplant: these plots accumulate shared diseases. Good drainage matters for strawberry; on damp sites make raised beds.

Planting
  • Timing: August–September or April.
  • Spacing of 25–30 cm in the row and 60–70 cm between rows.
  • The growing point (the "heart") must sit at soil level: neither buried nor raised.
  • Scatter GREENODIN BROWN over the bed before planting at 25 g per 1 m² and work it into the top layer.

Apply twice a year: in spring and once more during the season. The granules act over a long period. For a 100 m² plot, 25 g/m² is 2.5 kg.

Care through the season
  • Water at the root or by drip, without wetting leaves and berries.
  • Mulch the bed with straw or agrofibre: the berries stay clean.
  • After harvest, remove old leaves and surplus runners.
  • Renew the plantation every 3–4 years; after that the yield drops.
Which organic fertilizer is best for strawberry?

GREENODIN BROWN granular organic fertilizer for strawberry. Apply over the bed area at 25 g per 1 m² twice a year; the fertilizer improves soil structure and moisture retention.

How much fertilizer does strawberry need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to strawberry?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for strawberry?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is there scientific evidence of effectiveness?

Yes, the fertilizer has been tested by research institutions of the NAAS. 2019: the maximum yield of strawberry variety Rozana Kyivska was 66.2 c/ha with GREENODIN GRAY at 500 kg/ha broadcast and N45P30K15 fertigation. GREENODIN GRAY reduced the copper and zinc content of the berries by 23–30%. Reports are on the “Documents” tab.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
