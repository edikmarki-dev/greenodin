GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for apricot trees is an innovative organic granular fertilizer and soil conditioner based on leonardite and natural silica-bearing minerals, with a medium content of organic matter and a medium degree of mineralisation. GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral fertilizers for apricots are developed to international environmental standards, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

Growing apricots successfully with GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer

The apricot is one of the most valuable fruit crops, known for its refined flavour and high content of beneficial substances. To get a reliable crop, the tree needs good care and the right nutrition.

Apricots do best on soils with a neutral reaction. The new-generation GREENODIN GRAY organic apricot fertilizer helps achieve this: it enriches the soil with nutrients, stimulates root growth and increases yields.

Feeding apricots in spring: how and why to use GREENODIN GRAY

At the start of the season, trees need extra nutrition to begin growing and set next year's crop. Applying GREENODIN GRAY to apricots in spring provides:

  • Vigorous root system development thanks to humic and fulvic acids
  • Better availability of key macro- (N, P, K), meso- (Ca, Mg, S) and micronutrients
  • Higher resistance to temperature swings and spring frosts

Recommended application:

  • Spread 25 g/m² of fertilizer under the tree crown and work it into the soil to a depth of 5 cm, or simply leave it on the surface. Apply before or after rain, or with irrigation
Autumn feeding of apricot: preparing for winter with GREENODIN GRAY

In the second half of the season it is important to strengthen the roots and build up nutrient reserves for winter. Autumn application of GREENODIN GRAY for apricot:

  • Helps store energy for overwintering
  • Raises frost resistance thanks to nano-silicon
  • Improves soil structure and soil microflora activity
  • Method: the same as in spring - 25 g/m² under the crown, lightly incorporated and watered in
Feeding apricot saplings at planting

To help a young tree establish well, give it a starter supply of nutrients.

Before planting: add 50 g of GREENODIN GRAY to the planting hole and mix it with the soil.

After planting: add another 25 g over the root zone and cover with soil.

This approach promotes: faster rooting;

resistance to drought and disease;

better growth in the first months after planting.

NOTE: choose the planting site for apricot carefully, because its roots reach deep into the soil. Avoid sites where the water table lies deeper than 2 metres, and avoid acidic soils below pH 6.0, where beneficial microorganisms may be less active. This can be corrected by increasing the rate of GREENODIN GRAY for apricot 2-3 times to neutralize the acidity.

Why choose GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for apricots

Complete nutrition: all the key elements for active growth, including natural minerals (macro-, meso- and micronutrients), organic matter (peat, compost) and biologicals in the form of a consortium of beneficial microorganisms.

Stimulated root activity thanks to humic acids, fulvic acids and amino acids, which also improve the uptake of water and nutrients.

Protection against stress, as the nano-silicon it contains strengthens the tree's cell structures.

Better fruit quality: juicier, larger and longer-keeping.

Compatibility: can be combined with other fertilizers while avoiding an excess of chemicals that harm soil microflora.

Using GREENODIN GRAY for apricot simplifies tree care, because it solves several tasks at once:

  • long-lasting effect: it stays in the soil and builds an ecosystem, unlike liquid organic and, even more so, mineral fertilizers, which destroy it
  • accumulation, which lets you reduce the annual GREENODIN rate by 20% of the initial one without losing yield, and with continuous use from the 6th season on, apply only the minimum rate of 8 grams per square metre, or 80 kg per hectare at industrial scale
  • restoration and strengthening of natural soil fertility, with easily available mineral nutrition and natural humus formation
  • extra protection against known diseases, plus a stronger plant and a powerful root system thanks to nano-silicon
  • active work of agronomically useful bacteria, which suppress pathogenic flora and supply apricot with macro-, meso- and micronutrients in an easily absorbed form
  • up to 30% lower irrigation costs thanks to moisture retention
  • Using GREENODIN GRAY for apricot not only helps produce a rich harvest but also moves you toward sustainable farming, reducing dependence on chemical fertilizers and supporting the health of the soil and the environment
Which organic fertilizer is best for apricot trees?

GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for apricot trees. Apply in spring at the start of the season and once more later; it stimulates root growth and improves nutrient availability.

How much apricot fertilizer do I need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should I apply apricot fertilizer?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for apricots?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
