GREENODIN WHITE

GREENODIN WHITE organic fertilizer, ameliorant in granules, is an innovative complex organo-mineral granulated fertilizer, reclamation agent and ameliorant for sandy loam and loam soils based on potassium silicate, silicon-containing natural minerals and leonardite.

Purpose

Used to build up the water-holding capacity of soils. This is especially important in dry conditions, when water becomes a valuable resource that has to be saved.

Used to restore the physical structure of soils and raise fertility, the key factor behind high yields.

For sandy loam and loam soils, where water evaporates easily and nutrients are quickly washed out. GREENODIN WHITE holds moisture in the soil and preserves nutrients, so plants grow healthy and strong.

A strong water-holding capacity saves up to 40% of irrigation water. This conserves resources and makes irrigation more efficient.

A base fertilizer for farms and large areas. The line is chosen based on a soil analysis.

For use in extremely arid, even desert, regions with extreme abiotic factors

For use on soils with no or low content of organic matter and mineral nutrition elements

  • leonardite
  • potassium silicate
  • tripoli and diatomite are natural silicon-containing minerals with a high content of active silicon. They give the reclamation agent its high water-holding and ameliorative properties.
  • macro- and microelements needed for plant growth and for greater resistance to stress factors
  • agronomically beneficial microorganisms

Potassium (K) content, mg/100 g (control method per GOST 26718-85), not less than 200

The presence of bacteria has been confirmed by microbiological control.

Technical specifications: TU U 20.1-13856524-001:2014. Guaranteed shelf life of the packaged mixture is three years from the date of manufacture. Storage conditions: during transport, loading and unloading the product must be protected from precipitation. Store on sites protected from precipitation, groundwater and direct sunlight. The GREENODIN WHITE reclamation agent was developed by leading Ukrainian scientists. The product is certified under the Standard for the production of inputs permitted in organic agriculture and processing that comply with the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2018/848, certificate No. 23-0504-01/02 dated 16.10.2023. The cumulative effect of the product lets you lower the maximum rate from 250 kg/ha (25 g/sq. m) to 80 kg/ha (8 g/sq. m) by the 5th season of use

Applying GREENODIN White complex granulated organic fertilizers correctly matters a lot and helps raise soil fertility. Below are the main application methods.

Mixing the organic fertilizer into the soil mix

To use the GREENODIN White reclamation agent effectively in protected farming or in pots, we recommend mixing it into the soil mix. The fertilizer spreads evenly through the soil and works better.

Spreading the granulated GREENODIN White reclamation agent

In open-field farming, spread the GREENODIN White reclamation agent by hand or with mechanical equipment. For the nutrients to penetrate the soil quickly and evenly, it should be worked in to a depth of no more than 5–7 cm.

Watering the soil

After applying the GREENODIN White reclamation agent, it is recommended to water the soil so that the fertilizer spreads evenly across the whole area. Take the condition of the soil into account and water according to its moisture.

Choosing the time to apply the organic fertilizer

Spring and autumn are the most suitable times to apply the GREENODIN White reclamation agent. At those times the soil is still sufficiently moist and the air temperature is not too high, so the fertilizer works at its best. When choosing the time, however, take into account the specifics of the crop you grow.

What is GREENODIN WHITE used for?

GREENODIN WHITE is an ameliorant based on potassium silicate, tripoli, diatomite and leonardite with a high water-holding capacity. For sandy loam and loam soils in arid regions.

What is the application rate of GREENODIN WHITE per hectare?

The base rate is 250 kg/ha (25 g/m², 2.5 kg per 100 m²). Thanks to the cumulative effect the rate is cut by 20% every year, down to 80 kg/ha from the 5th–6th season. The exact rate depends on the soil analysis.

How do you apply GREENODIN WHITE in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are applied with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader before primary tillage or before sowing, after which the field is harrowed or disked.

What packaging does GREENODIN WHITE come in?

Polyethylene bags of 5 kg, polypropylene sacks of 25 and 50 kg. For farms and wholesale buyers, big bags of 600 to 1,100 kg.

Can different GREENODIN lines be combined?

Yes. Fertilizers from different GREENODIN lines can be combined depending on the acidity and composition of the soil. GREENODIN also lets you gradually reduce mineral fertilizers, up to giving them up completely.

Can it be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and animals?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.
