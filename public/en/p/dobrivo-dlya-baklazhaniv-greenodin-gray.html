The best organic granular fertilizer for growing eggplants outdoors and in greenhouses is GREENODIN GRAY. It contains a complex of macro- and micronutrients, has a pH of 7.0 and, in addition to natural minerals, includes organic nutrients that feed plants in a balanced way and improve the agronomic properties of the soil. Its humic and fulvic acids help plants make better use of soil nutrients and water.

GREENODIN GRAY increases eggplant yields by up to 60%, improves resistance to pests and diseases, saves 30% of water, raises soil fertility, supports beneficial soil microflora and improves root growth and development.

Mobile silicon increases eggplant resistance to pests, drought, heat, low temperatures and other stresses.

The ideal organic solution for eggplants is GREENODIN GRAY, which brings slightly acidic and slightly alkaline soils closer to neutral at a rate of 25 g/m², or 250 kg/ha at commercial scale.

Thanks to the cumulative action of every type of GREENODIN, the maximum rate of 25 g/m² (250 kg/ha) can be reduced to 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) by the 6th season by cutting it by 20% each season with no loss of effectiveness, which means substantial savings.

Where to grow eggplants

To grow eggplants successfully, choose a site that meets these requirements:

  • Sun. Eggplants need plenty of sunlight to grow and fruit properly. Choose a spot that gets direct sun for most of the day, at least 6–8 hours.
  • Warmth. Eggplants are heat-loving plants and need enough warmth to grow and develop. Choose a place where the temperature stays in their optimal range, usually 24–30 °C.
  • No shade. Make sure the site is not shaded by large trees, walls or other structures that block the sun and limit light.
  • Spacing between plants: leave enough distance between eggplant plants for proper growth and development. A gap of 60-90 cm is recommended, to give them room to spread and ensure access to light and air.

GREENODIN GRAY is designed to smooth out various negative factors as much as possible at the recommended dose of 25 grams per square meter or 250 kilograms per hectare in commercial conditions. It is made to improve plant growth and nutrient uptake, strengthen plants against stress, and protect against diseases and pests. With GREENODIN GRAY you can optimize plant health and reduce the negative impact of adverse conditions on yield.

The beneficial microflora in GREENODIN GRAY (nitrogen-fixing, phosphate- and potassium-mobilising and silicon-modifying bacteria) improves the uptake of even hard-to-reach soil nutrients, and Bacillus subtilis (hay bacillus) further suppresses pathogenic microflora.

Soil for eggplants

The soil should have a good structure that supports healthy root growth, be well aerated and contain enough organic matter to hold moisture and nutrients. Make sure the site drains well, as eggplants do not tolerate waterlogging, and good drainage helps prevent root rot. Eggplants need fertile soil to grow and fruit well. Prepare the soil by adding GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer to improve its fertility and nutrient content.

Caring for eggplants

A large, high-quality eggplant crop needs proper care, including:

Watering eggplants

Eggplants need regular watering. Keep the soil evenly moist and avoid both drying out and waterlogging. GREENODIN GRAY applied before watering retains moisture, saves 30% of irrigation water and makes it last longer. Water deeply so the roots get enough, 1–2 litres per plant.

Feeding eggplants

Feed eggplants regularly with GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for healthy growth and fruiting. It makes an ideal feed thanks to the nitrogen-fixing, potassium- and phosphate-mobilising bacteria and other beneficial microorganisms it contains. They supply eggplants with macro- and micronutrients, including mobile silicon, which greatly strengthens the plant, protects it from many diseases and helps prevent pest attacks.

Training eggplant plants

Remove side shoots and excess leaves, especially those growing close to the ground, to prevent disease and let air and light reach the lower part of the plant. Pruning keeps the plant compact and increases yields. Make sure the main stem is straight and stable.

Staking eggplants

If the plants and their fruit are large, tie them to a support to avoid damaging stems and fruit. Use soft materials such as straw or special woven ties.

Controlling weeds around eggplants

Weed control around eggplants is an important part of care that gives them good growing conditions. Pull weeds by hand from around the plants, taking care not to damage the eggplant roots. Mow grass and weeds around the eggplants regularly to stop them spreading, especially before they flower and set seed. Chopped clippings make useful mulch.

Mulching eggplants

Spread a 5–7 cm layer of mulch around the base of the plant, leaving a small gap around the stem so it is not smothered. Mulch retains moisture, reduces competition from weeds and protects roots from temperature swings. The best mulch is made from chopped straw, dry grass (including weeds) and lower leaves, with GREENODIN added. Check the mulch from time to time and top it up as needed.

Protecting eggplants from pests

Watch for pests, especially aphids, whitefly and Colorado potato beetles, and take timely action using organic products. The mobile silicon that eggplants absorb from GREENODIN GRAY strengthens the plants and makes them unattractive to pests.

Which organic fertilizer is best for eggplants?

GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for eggplants grown outdoors and in greenhouses. Apply when preparing the bed or planting out seedlings; it releases nutrients gradually through the season.

How much eggplant fertilizer do I need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should I apply eggplant fertilizer?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for eggplants?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
