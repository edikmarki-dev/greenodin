GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer for apple trees in granules is a modern complex granulated organo-mineral fertilizer, soil reclamation agent and ameliorant based on silicon-containing natural minerals and leonardite with added peat.

GREENODIN BROWN complex granulated organic fertilizers for apple trees are developed to international environmental requirements, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

Benefits of GREENODIN BROWN Complex Granulated Fertilizers for Apple Trees

GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer for apple trees contains various components, including the macro- and microelements needed for plant growth and resistance to stress, as well as agronomically beneficial microorganisms.

GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer for apple trees raises soil fertility and improves its agronomic properties and biological indicators. It helps to raise apple yield by up to 65% and to strengthen plant vitality and resistance to disease.

Another advantage of GREENODIN BROWN is that it reduces the use of agrochemicals, even to the point of phasing them out completely. This makes it possible to switch to organic farming and improve the quality and market value of clean produce.

Importantly, GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer is completely safe for people, bees and animals, which makes it especially attractive for farming and gardening.

A healthy, abundant apple harvest comes from proper feeding of the trees throughout the year, split into 4 seasons. GREENODIN is an effective solution for every season.

The best fertilizer with the optimal acidity of pH 5.0 is GREENODIN BROWN, which can be used on all soils.

Spring feeding of apple treesFrom March 10 to April 15, when young leaves unfold, apply GREENODIN BROWN fertilizer so that apple trees recover after winter, get stronger against pests and diseases and have reserves for the whole growing period.

Summer feeding of apple trees

At the end of June, after deep loosening and abundant watering of the soil, use GREENODIN to support the growth and health of the trees. This feeding secures high results in both the quantity and quality of the harvest.

Autumn feeding of apple treesAt the turn of August and September prepare fruit trees for winter with GREENODIN BROWN, including by replacing the nutrients given up for the harvest and for the growth of the tree. Feeding apple trees before winter

In late September and early October strengthen apple trees before the cold weather, paying attention to preserving soil fertility and structure, including its biota.

Use and application rates of the complex organo-mineral fertilizer for apple trees GREENODIN BROWN: 30 g per sq. m, lightly worked in and watered if the soil is dry, applied 10 cm from the trunk across the diameter of the crown. Regular and correct use of GREENODIN organic fertilizers will help you grow healthy, fruitful apple trees.

Remember that GREENODIN organic fertilizers are living and self-regulating and have no side effects, so happily you can't have too much of a good thing!

Which organic fertilizer is best for apple trees?

GREENODIN BROWN fertilizer for apple trees: 25 g per 1 liter of soil when planting a sapling, then per tree depending on its height. The prolonged action lets you cut the rate by 20% every year.

How much fertilizer do apple trees need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

At planting, 25 g per 1 L of soil or substrate. For plants 0.5–1 m tall, 50 g per plant; for 1–1.5 m, 75 g. Apply twice a year, reducing the rate by 20% each year.

When should you apply fertilizer to apple trees?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for apple trees?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
