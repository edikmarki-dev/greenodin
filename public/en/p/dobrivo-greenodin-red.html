GREENODIN RED

GREENODIN RED organic fertilizer, ameliorant, is an innovative complex organo-mineral granulated fertilizer based on brown bentonite, silicon-containing natural minerals and lignite.

Purpose

Increases the water-holding capacity of soils

Restores the physical structure of soils and increases their water and air permeability. Raises fertility

Speeds up plant growth thanks to agronomically beneficial bacteria that fix and mobilize macroelements (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium) and microelements (boron, zinc, manganese, molybdenum, etc.), providing natural nutrition in a form plants take up easily

Strengthens the biochemical defense of plants and raises their resistance to stress such as drought, frost, strong winds and extreme temperatures

Improves the uptake of microelements by plants. The humic and fulvic acids in the organic fertilizer help plants absorb microelements, which raises their yield and quality.

In farming, the way the GREENODIN RED reclamation agent is applied matters. It supplies plants with the nutrients they need and raises soil fertility. The main steps for applying the fertilizer correctly are described below.

Mixing GREENODIN RED reclamation agent into the soil mix

For protected farming and use in pots, we recommend mixing the GREENODIN RED reclamation agent into the soil mix for maximum efficiency. This distributes the nutrients evenly through the soil and makes them work better.

Spreading GREENODIN RED reclaimer

In open-field farming GREENODIN RED reclaimer should be spread by hand or with mechanical equipment. It is recommended to incorporate it to a depth of no more than 5-7 cm to ensure fast, even penetration of nutrients into the soil.

Watering the soil

After applying the GREENODIN RED reclamation agent, it is recommended to water the soil so that the fertilizer dissolves quickly and spreads evenly across the whole crop area. It is important to take the condition of the soil into account and to water according to its moisture.

Choosing the time for application

We recommend applying the GREENODIN RED reclamation agent in spring and autumn, when the soil is still sufficiently moist and the air temperature is not too high, so that the fertilizer works at its best. When choosing the time, take into account the crop you grow and its specifics.

Keeping to the dosage

When applying the GREENODIN RED reclamation agent, it is important to keep to the dosage given on the package. This gives plants the optimal amount of nutrients and helps to get the most out of the complex organic fertilizer.

What is GREENODIN RED used for?

GREENODIN RED is a soil conditioner based on brown bentonite, lignite and silica-bearing minerals for increasing the fertility of acidic, depleted soils.

What is the application rate of GREENODIN RED per hectare?

The base rate is 250 kg/ha (25 g/m², 2.5 kg per 100 m²). Thanks to the cumulative effect the rate is cut by 20% every year, down to 80 kg/ha from the 5th–6th season. The exact rate depends on the soil analysis.

How do you apply GREENODIN RED in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are applied with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader before primary tillage or before sowing, after which the field is harrowed or disked.

What packaging does GREENODIN RED come in?

Polyethylene bags of 5 kg, polypropylene sacks of 25 and 50 kg. For farms and wholesale buyers, big bags of 600 to 1,100 kg.

Which crops is GREENODIN RED suitable for?

Barley, corn, soybeans, roses.

Can different GREENODIN lines be combined?

Yes. Fertilizers from different GREENODIN lines can be combined depending on the acidity and composition of the soil. GREENODIN also lets you gradually reduce mineral fertilizers, up to giving them up completely.

Can it be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and animals?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.
