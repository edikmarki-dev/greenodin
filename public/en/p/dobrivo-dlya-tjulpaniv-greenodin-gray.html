GREENODIN GRAY complex organic fertilizer for tulips in granules is a modern complex granular organo-mineral fertilizer, soil restorer and conditioner based on silica-bearing natural minerals and leonardite, with a medium organic content.

GREENODIN GRAY organo-mineral complex granular fertilizers for tulips are developed in line with international environmental requirements, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified by Organic Standard.

Features and benefits of GREENODIN GRAY complex organic granular fertilizers for tulips

Safety

GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for tulips is completely safe for people, bees and animals.

Complete nutrition

GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizer for tulips supplies plants with all the necessary macronutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium) and micronutrients (boron, iron, manganese, copper, molybdenum and zinc) in an easily available form for healthy, natural development. NOTE: the amount of NPK the plants need is provided by agronomically beneficial bacteria (nitrogen-fixing, phosphate-mobilising and potassium-mobilising) that are part of GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer

Nitrogen (N) boosts the growth and development of peonies.

Potassium (K) increases resistance to diseases and stress factors and aids nitrogen fixation.

Phosphorus (P) stimulates root system development and the formation of flower buds, and extends the flowering period

Calcium (Ca) improves soil structure and the uptake of other elements, speeds up the development of roots and leaves, and increases immunity to harmful organisms and fungal infections

Molybdenum (Mo) promotes root system growth and speeds up vegetation

Boron (B) stimulates the growing points of young plants, improves the uptake of useful substances and increases resistance to fungal and bacterial diseases

The balanced composition of GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for tulips promotes faster growth, a strong root system, stronger “immunity” and abundant, long-lasting flowering. Your plants will delight you with bright, rich colours, adding beauty and appeal.

Granular form

GREENODIN GRAY granules ensure even nutrient distribution and ease of use. Simply scatter the granules around the peony bush and water them so that the fertilizer starts releasing its beneficial microorganisms.

Regular use of the fertilizer helps ensure healthy and correct growth of your plants.

Over-fertilising with GREENODIN GRAY complex organo-mineral fertilizer for tulips is impossible.

Below are the benefits in more detail that you get from using GREENODIN GRAY complex organo-mineral granular fertilizer for tulips

Which organic fertilizer is best for tulips?

GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for tulips. Apply to the soil before planting the bulbs; the prolonged action sustains the plants' nutrition until flowering.

How much fertilizer do tulips need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to tulips?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for tulips?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
