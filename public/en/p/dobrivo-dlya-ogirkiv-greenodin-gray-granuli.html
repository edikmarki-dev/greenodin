GREENODIN GRAY granular organic fertilizer for cucumber is an ideal, universal product for growing cucumbers. It promotes healthy cucumber growth, restores fertility and enriches the soil, improving its structure. Thanks to its composition and many functions, GREENODIN GRAY is a complex fertilizer and also a full green manure for cucumbers. Put simply, GREENODIN GRAY biological fertilizer consists of an organic part in the form of leonardite and/or peat and a mineral part in the form of the silica-rich natural mineral diatomite or tripoli.

This mix is activated by organic polysilicic acids and agronomically useful microorganisms.

GREENODIN GRAY works effectively as a fertilizer for cucumbers both in open ground and in greenhouses. Fertilizing cucumbers in greenhouses differs only in where it is applied; the rates and methods are the same. Apply 25 grams per square meter for base application, or per running meter when planting in a row. For local application, 25 g under each plant.

By using GREENODIN GRAY complex fertilizer for cucumbers, both as a base fertilizer and for feeding, you are guaranteed:

  • Higher soil fertility, with improved agronomic properties and biological indicators.
  • Lower soil toxicity and restrained development of phytopathogenic microorganisms.
  • Stronger plant vitality and greater resistance to disease.
  • Stimulated plant growth and development.
  • Up to 30% less irrigation water.
  • A 20% reduction in the application rate every year.
  • Reduced use of agrochemicals, even to the point of abandoning them completely.
  • A transition to organic farming technologies.
  • Higher quality value and market price of the organic cucumbers you grow.
Soil for cucumbers

The optimal soil acidity for cucumbers is around neutral pH 7.0, starting from slightly acidic pH 6.5. This range provides the best conditions for cucumber growth and development.

If the soil pH is too low (acidic) and below 6.5, add GREENODIN GRAY to raise the pH, reduce soil acidity and bring it closer to the optimal level.

If the soil pH is too high (alkaline) and above 7.0, add GREENODIN BROWN to lower the pH and increase soil acidity, bringing it closer to the optimal range.

Before planting cucumbers it is recommended to test the soil pH, as advised in the article “Determining soil acidity”.

It is important to remember that cucumbers tolerate small deviations from the optimal pH, but extreme values and excesses harm nutrient uptake and lead to problems with growth and development. Maintaining the optimal soil pH is therefore an important part of growing cucumbers successfully.

What soil do cucumbers like? Cucumbers prefer loose, fertile soil with good drainage, rich in organic matter, with a neutral pH of 6.5–7.0 and sufficiently moist but not waterlogged.

It is important to keep the soil moist by watering the plants regularly, especially in dry periods. Cucumbers can also be grown in pots or containers, where you can control the soil composition and provide optimal growing conditions.

Besides the soil, it is also important to give cucumbers enough light, warmth and protection from cold draughts.

Planting cucumbers

Planting cucumbers outdoors means choosing a sunny spot in your garden or vegetable plot where they get a full day of light. They love warmth, so choose a place with a good thermal regime.

Prepare the soil as described in the “Soil for cucumbers” section: open up the beds, dig the soil, remove weeds and apply GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for cucumbers, 25 g per linear metre in the row.

Cucumbers can be grown from seed or from transplants. If you use seed, sow it about 1–2 cm deep, following the recommendations on the seed packet. If you use transplants, plant them in the beds at the recommended spacing between plants (usually about 30–45 cm).

How far apart should cucumbers be planted? The distance depends on the variety, the type of cucumber and the space available:

  • For vertical cucumber growing (with a support or trellis): 60-90 cm between rows, 30-45 cm between plants in the row.
  • For horizontal growing along the ground or a bed: 90-120 cm between rows, 45-60 cm between plants in the row.

Near the sowings or planted-out seedlings, set up supports such as lattices or trellises to help plants climb and hold the fruit, since cucumbers are vines. These recommendations give cucumbers enough room to grow, access to sunlight and good air circulation between plants.

After planting in open or protected ground, follow the rules for keeping the soil moist and remove weeds and pests. When planting cucumbers in a greenhouse, follow the conditions above for vertical growing.

Planting cucumbers in May means that a warmer season is coming and the soil is starting to heat up. Make sure the soil has warmed to about 15–20 °C. Cold soil will slow cucumber growth and can cause problems. Sow cucumber seed or plant transplants about 1–2 cm deep, observing the recommended spacing between plants.

Planting cucumbers in July means a hotter time of year, so protect cucumbers from direct sun and excessive drought. Prepare the soil, sow seed or plant transplants at the spacings described above, and water the plants regularly, especially in dry spells, to give them enough moisture.

Extra protection from extreme heat may be needed: use shading or sun-protection netting.

Growing cucumbers

Growing cucumbers requires fertile soil with good drainage. The soil should be loose and contain organic matter. Prepare the beds, dig the soil and remove weeds.

Cucumbers need regular watering. Keep the soil moist, especially in hot weather. Water in the morning or evening to prevent moisture from evaporating. Avoid overwatering the soil so the roots do not rot.

Cucumbers are vines and need support to grow vertically. Install supports such as nets or trellises next to the plants to help them climb. Remove weeds regularly to avoid competition for nutrients.

Regular feeding helps cucumbers develop and bear fruit. On open ground, use GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizers for cucumbers, which contain a large amount of macro- and micronutrients.

Feeding cucumbers

Feeding cucumbers helps them get the nutrients they need and stimulates their growth and development. An ideal option is GREENODIN GRAY organic fertilizers for cucumbers, which provide a pH of 6.5–7.0 and a supply of macro- and micronutrients that improve soil fertility and give the plants the nutrition they need. Place the organic fertilizer around the plants and gently work it into the soil.

Feed cucumbers regularly throughout their growth period, applying the indicated fertilizers together with watering. The first feeding is recommended 2–3 weeks after planting, and then repeat it every 2–3 weeks. Watch the condition of the cucumbers and how they respond to feeding. If you notice signs of a deficiency of any nutrient (for example, yellowing leaves), apply the appropriate fertilizer.

Remember that feeding is a supplement to proper soil preparation and regular watering. Take into account the individual needs and recommendations for your cucumber variety and local conditions to get a good harvest.

Which organic fertilizer is best for cucumbers?

GREENODIN GRAY fertilizer for cucumbers outdoors and in greenhouses. The silica component helps retain moisture, and the beneficial bacteria mobilise nutrients for the plants.

How much fertilizer do cucumbers need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to cucumbers?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN GRAY recommended for cucumbers?

GREENODIN GRAY is the base line for most soils: it restores fertility, improves soil structure and has a medium water-holding capacity.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
