GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer is an excellent choice for bougainvillea, which usually needs constant feeding because of its vigorous growth: it grows all year except in winter. With a pH of 5.0–6.0, the sweet spot for this plant, GREENODIN BROWN provides the nutrients it needs while supporting and strengthening the soil ecosystem. The leonardite and diatomite in GREENODIN BROWN structure the soil, improving drainage and moisture retention. This is especially useful for bougainvillea, which needs good drainage and evenly moist soil.

The beneficial microorganisms support root development and overall plant health. They break down organic matter, make nutrients more available and increase disease resistance. Use GREENODIN BROWN according to the manufacturer's instructions to maintain optimal levels of beneficial microorganisms in the soil. GREENODIN BROWN bougainvillea fertilizer keeps your plant healthy and, as a result, keeps its owner happy.

What is bougainvillea

Bougainvillea is a tropical flowering plant found in many warm countries, such as Brazil, Peru, Argentina and Mexico. It is known for its bright bracts and dense green foliage. Its small, inconspicuous flowers are surrounded by vivid bracts in red, pink, purple, orange and many other colours, which makes it very popular for decorating gardens and landscapes. Bougainvillea can grow as a climber, a shrub or even a small tree and is easy to shape by pruning. At home it is usually grown as a shrub.

Growing bougainvillea

Grow bougainvillea at 20–30 °C in a sunny spot where it gets direct sunlight for most of the day, and follow the essential care guidelines. This keeps it healthy and flowering well.

Soil for bougainvillea

Bougainvillea grows in soil with a pH of 5.5–6.5, from slightly acidic to neutral, where it can absorb a wide range of nutrients efficiently. If the pH is too low or too high, the plant cannot take up nutrients properly, which leads to growth problems and can trigger disease. Bougainvillea soil should be light, nutritious and free-draining, with a neutral or slightly acidic reaction. Pots for bougainvillea: if you grow it in a pot, aim for slightly acidic to neutral soil with a pH of about 6.0.

If you want to change the soil pH, you can use dedicated soil acidity products from garden centres. Choose a pot that looks a little tight for the plant; this prevents the sudden, uncontrolled growth that often happens in large containers. We recommend 2–3 litre pots and moving up only when the plant is clearly pot-bound. A drainage hole and a layer of expanded clay ensure good drainage. Fill the pot with soil and then enrich and feed it with GREENODIN BROWN bougainvillea fertilizer.

Caring for bougainvillea

Follow these simple but very important care tips and you will have a bright plant that flowers almost all year round.

Light for bougainvillea

Bougainvillea needs bright sunlight to flower well. Place it in a sunny spot with direct sun for most of the day. The mobile silicon in GREENODIN BROWN helps the plant keep its vivid colour even in direct sunlight.

Watering bougainvillea

Water bougainvillea regularly but avoid overwatering. Space out watering so the soil stays moist but not soggy. Well-drained soil helps prevent standing water. GREENODIN BROWN retains moisture, which is very handy if you forget to water the plant.

Soil for bougainvillea

Use well-drained soil for bougainvillea. You can add sand or perlite to improve drainage. It is also important for the soil to contain enough organic matter.

Feeding bougainvillea

Bougainvillea should be fed with fertilizers for flowering plants that contain a range of macro- and micronutrients, and GREENODIN BROWN is ideal for this: 25 g per plant, or dissolved in water a few hours before watering.

Supporting bougainvillea

If your bougainvillea has a climbing habit, give it support such as stakes or horizontal wires. This helps it grow in the right direction and keep its shape. GREENODIN fertilizers containing mobile silicon make the plant sturdier, so it holds its shape better. The silicon and bacteria in the fertilizer also make the plant more resistant to pests and diseases, helping prevent them.

Pruning bougainvillea

Pruning is important for keeping bougainvillea healthy and in shape. Do it after the period of active growth and flowering, usually in late winter or early spring. Avoid pruning in autumn or winter, as you may lose flowers. Remove dead and diseased branches, which do nothing for the plant and can spread infection. Prune to adjust the plant's shape and size. It is best to keep its natural shape, but you can also train it into decorative forms.

If your bougainvillea is too big for its space, cut it back to about a third of the length of each branch. After hard pruning, feed it with 25 g of GREENODIN BROWN, either dissolved in water or spread under the plant and watered in well.

How much bougainvillea fertilizer do I need per pot or per 1 L of substrate?

25 g per 1 L of substrate when planting or repotting. Mix the granules into the potting mix. The following year, reduce the rate by 20%.

When should I apply bougainvillea fertilizer?

When planting or repotting: mix the granules into the substrate. After that, the slow-release action keeps the plant fed.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Measure out the right amount of granules, mix them evenly into the potting mix, plant and water.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for bougainvillea?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
