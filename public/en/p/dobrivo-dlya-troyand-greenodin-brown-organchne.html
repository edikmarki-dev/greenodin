Growing roses. Modern methods and generally accepted norms. The ideal soil for roses is a slightly acidic loam (pH 5.8-6.5). To keep the soil from being heavy, loosen it with peat, agroperlite or vermiculite, and if there is a lot of sand, add compost or peat. If the soil pH is below 5.5, dolomite at 200-300 g is recommended. Attention! Benefits of GREENODIN™ organic fertilizers for roses

Ten years of creating and refining the best fertilizer for roses has produced the ideal form and composition:

  • Form - organic fertilizers that are dry, free-flowing and granular, some as a substrate, but in every case they stay in the soil and do not evaporate or break down into dirty compounds like chemical fertilizers

Composition: a balanced organo-mineral one, where the mineral component is diatomite (amorphous silica), which levels the pH depending on the type of soil and the fertilizer itself. For example, to acidify neutral (pH 7.0) artificial store-bought soil, it is enough to add GREENODIN BROWN (pH 5.0) at 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate. The pH drops to roughly 6.0, creating useful conditions for active rose growth. In addition, diatomite acts as a buffer for storing and structuring water, which cannot be evaporated by the sun's rays but is easily absorbed by the rose through the capillaries of its roots.

It is no accident that silica gel, which is also made from silicon, is used to remove excess moisture from food products.

The other component of GREENODIN is organic: leonardite with peat, enriched with humic and fulvic acids, which also affect pH but, most importantly, serve as food for microorganisms and for the rose itself. One of the main elements is the complex of agronomically beneficial bacteria in GREENODIN, which supply the rose with macro- and micronutrients. Some of the bacteria suppress the development of pathogenic flora and, as a result, prevent rose diseases.

The main action of GREENODIN complex organic fertilizers is to renew or raise the fertility of the soils in which roses are grown. So if you happened to plant a rose in old or garden soil, do not worry. Just add GREENODIN. If the soil is too acidic, pH 5.5 or below, it is enough to add neutral GREENODIN GRAY or alkaline GREENODIN RED at 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate (soil).

Silicon in the right form, combined with humic and fulvic acid, has a very positive effect on the rose's plant characteristics. The stem wall becomes stronger and the colour of the flowers and of the rose as a whole becomes richer.

Thanks to their composition and the absence of negative aftereffects, GREENODIN™ fertilizers are completely safe for people, pets and bees, so they can be used safely right on apiaries, in gardens, vegetable plots, around the house and when growing medicinal plants.

Application rates and planting tips

Application advice falls into two cases:

Planting a rose in new soil

If you do not have soil or want to create ideal growing conditions for roses right away, all you need is to provide soil acidity that matches the rose's needs (pH 5.8–6.5).

To do this, prepare a flower substrate and add GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer (pH 5.0) at 25-30 g per 1 l of substrate.

Then mix, put into a pot, tray or hole and water. If planting in a garden plot, the prepared mixture is poured in a mound at the bottom of the hole.

Water immediately and, in high daytime temperatures, in the morning or evening.

Nothing more is required of you: enjoy the growth and flowering.

Planting a rose in existing soil

You should achieve the soil acidity suited to roses (pH 5.8–6.5), and then fertilize depending on its acidity.

Apply the fertilizer, mix it with your soil and water it in.

Caring for a rose after planting

Care of the rose after planting: regular watering, because roses are sensitive to a lack of soil moisture:

  • shaping the bush with scissors and garden pruning shears
  • feeding with GREENODIN BLACK at 25-30 g per 1 litre of substrate or per 1 sq. m of garden
  • winter preparation (in the garden)
  • loosening the soil and weeding (in the garden)
  • control of pests and diseases

Which organic fertilizer is best for roses?

GREENODIN BROWN organic fertilizer for garden and indoor roses. For pots, 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate; in the garden, over the area of the flower bed.

How much fertilizer do roses need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

25 g per 1 m², which is 2.5 kg per 100 m² or 250 kg/ha, twice a year. From year 2 to year 4 the rate is reduced by 20% each year, and from year 5 you apply 8 g/m² (0.8 kg per 100 m²).

When should fertilizer be applied to roses?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for roses?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Are other GREENODIN lines suitable for roses?

Yes: GREENODIN BLACK for feeding: 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate or per 1 m²; GREENODIN GRAY for neutral soils, 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate; GREENODIN RED for acidic soils, 25–30 g per 1 litre of substrate.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
