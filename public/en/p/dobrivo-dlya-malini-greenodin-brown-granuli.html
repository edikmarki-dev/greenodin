Raspberry grows well where the soil is loose, rich in organic matter and not waterlogged. The site and timely pruning do more for it than any feeding, and the fertilizer consolidates the result.

Site and soil

Raspberry needs a bright, draft-free spot. The optimal soil acidity is pH 5.7–6.5. Heavy clay and waterlogged areas are unsuitable: raspberry roots become waterlogged and die. The water table should lie no closer than 1–1.5 m to the surface.

Planting
  • Plant saplings in autumn or early spring.
  • Space bushes 40–50 cm apart in the row, rows 1.5–2 m apart.
  • Keep the root collar at soil level; after planting water well and mulch.
  • Add GREENODIN BROWN to the planting hole at 25 g per 1 l of soil and mix it with the earth.

Apply twice a year. The fertilizer acts over a long period, so the rate is reduced by 20% every year. Scatter the granules under the bush and water in; you can also cover them with soil.

Care through the season
  • In spring, before the buds open, remove weak and broken shoots and tie the rest to the trellis.
  • Do not let the soil dry out during flowering and berry growth: raspberry responds to even watering.
  • After harvest, cut the fruited two-year-old canes down to the ground.
  • In autumn, mulch the strip under the bushes with peat, compost or straw.
Which organic fertilizer is best for raspberry?

GREENODIN BROWN fertilizer for raspberry. At planting, 25 g per 1 litre of soil; for mature bushes, per plant depending on height; the effect is prolonged.

How much fertilizer does raspberry need per 100 m² or per 1 m²?

At planting, 25 g per 1 L of soil or substrate. For plants 0.5–1 m tall, 50 g per plant; for 1–1.5 m, 75 g. Apply twice a year, reducing the rate by 20% each year.

When should fertilizer be applied to raspberry?

Twice a year: in spring, when preparing the soil, sowing or planting, and once more during the season. The granules can be applied in spring, summer or autumn; their slow-release action keeps feeding plants throughout the season.

How do I apply granular fertilizer correctly?

Apply to the root zone: scatter the granules over the soil surface or around the trunk and water them in; you can lightly cover them with soil.

Why is GREENODIN BROWN recommended for raspberry?

GREENODIN BROWN has an acidic reaction (pH about 5.0) and acidifies the soil. This is what plants that struggle in alkaline and neutral conditions need.

Is the fertilizer safe for people, bees and pets?

Yes. The fertilizer is safe for people, bees and animals and contains no synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

Can the fertilizer be used in organic farming?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.

How long does the fertilizer keep?

3 years after opening. Store in a dry place protected from rain and direct sunlight.
