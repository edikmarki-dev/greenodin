GREENODIN fertilizers for barley

GREENODIN barley fertilizers are organo-mineral granular fertilizers that maintain soil fertility and feed spring and winter barley. They improve soil conditions and support beneficial microflora and nutrient mobilisation. The choice between GREENODIN RED, GRAY and BROWN depends on soil pH. The manufacturer's base rate is 250 kg/ha.

Barley fertilizers

For efficient barley production, use GREENODIN organic fertilizers. They deliver: higher yields; better soil structure; more nutrients in the soil; active soil microorganisms; and improved soil water and air regimes.

To raise barley yield and quality while keeping the produce environmentally clean, apply GREENODIN barley fertilizer during primary tillage before sowing. The first step is discing the stubble after stubble crops, and the second is ploughing. In areas prone to wind erosion, additional tillage with green manure crops is used. Snow retention is needed in winter.

Spring tillage includes pre-sowing cultivation with general-purpose cultivators and harrowing with chain-linked tine harrows to conserve soil moisture. GREENODIN granular fertilizers enhance soil fertility, so applying them at the same time makes good sense. How to get good soil for barley: successful farming means restoring and increasing soil fertility. The optimal soil pH for barley, both organic and integrated, is 6.8–7.5. Growing barley on acidic, waterlogged soils is not recommended.

Low yields are guaranteed on saline and light sandy soils with little organic matter. However, these shortcomings can be offset with Organic Standard-certified GREENODIN organic granular fertilizers. For soils with pH around 7.5, GREENODIN BROWN is very useful. For soils with pH 6.5 and below, GREENODIN RED works effectively. And at pH 6.5–7.5, GREENODIN GRAY gives excellent results.

To get good barley yields while renewing and increasing soil fertility, it is very important to create conditions that supply plants with macro- and micronutrients. The beneficial bacteria in GREENODIN organic granular fertilizers do this very well. Growing barley: organic barley, like conventional barley, is grown as a spring crop, but in Ukraine and many other countries there is now a shift towards winter barley.

Best predecessors for organic barley. The best predecessors for winter barley are crops sown into well-fertilized soil, such as peas, fibre flax or broken perennial grassland. Pulses deserve special mention, as they leave plenty of nitrogen in the soil, making it worthwhile to sow barley for feed and food afterwards. The best predecessors for spring barley are row crops and pulses, including sugar beet, grain maize, peas and soybeans. Barley in turn is a good predecessor for sugar beet. After the previous crop is harvested, the field must be thoroughly cleared of weeds.

Any predecessor benefits organic barley as long as the crop residues are broken down properly, which is naturally achieved with Trichoderma-based microbial products or the organic product ECOSTERN. Organic barley production technology: the fertilization plan for winter barley in organic and integrated farming is as follows: before sowing, treat the seed with HELPROST® at 2 L/t in 10 L of water and MIKOFREND® (mycorrhiza) at 1.5 L/t in 10 L of water; during primary tillage or at sowing (as the farmer prefers), apply 250 kg/ha of GREENODIN fertilizer, choosing the type according to soil pH.

On Ukrainian soils, GREENODIN GRAY is the universal solution. GREENODIN fertilizers contain nitrogen-fixing, phosphate-solubilising and potassium-mobilising microorganisms.

We recommend applying organic fertilizer to the crop before barley as well, to speed up biochemical processes and activate enzymes in the plants, increasing barley's resistance to disease and external stress such as drought, cold and intense sunlight. Organic fertilizer rate for barley: the overall rate depends on many soil characteristics, but the starting rate is 250 kg of GREENODIN organic fertilizer per hectare, or 2.5 kg per 100 m², or 25 g per 1 m².

The cumulative effect of GREENODIN products means the dose goes down every season.

Subsequent applications allow the maximum rate to be reduced from 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) to 80 kg/ha (8 g/m²) by the 6th season on the same field. Feeding organic barley: for healthy, strong growth, barley needs proper nutrition, and the beneficial bacteria in GREENODIN organic fertilizers handle this very well. They fix and mobilise macronutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium) and micronutrients (boron, zinc, manganese, molybdenum and others), giving barley plenty of natural nutrition in an easily absorbed form:

  • Phosphorus (P) is needed for better tillering, prevents lodging and improves grain quality. The phosphorus-producing bacteria contained in GREENODIN handle this task very well

Potassium (K) affects moisture retention in the soil and works together with silicon in its amorphous form. Potassium combined with silicon strongly influences yield, increases seed weight and protein content, supports nitrogen fixation and raises resistance to disease and stress, drought included. All of this is reinforced by the potassium-producing bacteria in GREENODIN

Nitrogen (N) stimulates the growth and development of barley. Science has shown that cereals cover 60–70% of their nitrogen needs through symbiosis with nitrogen-fixing bacteria, which are plentiful in GREENODIN organic fertilizers

Calcium (Ca) improves soil structure, the uptake of other elements and the development of the barley root system, and it is part of GREENODIN. Calcium takes part in hormonal reactions and, together with silicon, in building strong barley stem walls that protect against pests

Magnesium (Mg) raises yield, chlorophyll content and barley's resistance to disease, and supports enzyme processes and fat accumulation; Boron (B) stimulates the growth points of young barley plants, partly through nitrogen supply

Zinc (Zn) activates a number of enzymes and takes part in plant nitrogen metabolism and protein formation;

Manganese (Mn) activates enzymes, chlorophyll synthesis and photosynthesis in general;

Molybdenum (Mo) promotes root growth, activates beneficial nodule bacteria and speeds up vegetation. It accumulates in young plant organs and, at the end of the season, concentrates in the seeds, raising yield and protein content. GREENODIN organo-mineral complex granular fertilizers are developed to international environmental requirements, comply with Regulation (EU) 2018/848 and are certified to Organic Standard.

Which organic fertilizer is best for barley?

GREENODIN RED, GREENODIN GRAY and GREENODIN BROWN are recommended for barley. The line is chosen according to soil acidity and composition, and fertilizers can be combined.

What is the fertilizer application rate for barley per hectare?

The main application is 250 kg/ha before primary tillage or at sowing. The minimum rate is down to 80 kg/ha by season 6.

When should fertilizer be applied to barley?

Before primary tillage or at sowing. After harvest, on stubble or crop residues: 150 kg/ha of GRAY on stubble + ECOSTERN 1.5 L/ha.

How do I choose a GREENODIN line for barley based on soil pH?

pH 6.5 and below — GREENODIN RED; pH 6.5–7.5 — GREENODIN GRAY; pH 7.5 and above — GREENODIN BROWN. Soil acidity is best determined by a lab test.

What soil pH is optimal for barley?

The optimal soil reaction is pH 6.8–7.5.

What are the best preceding crops for barley?

Winter barley: peas, fibre flax, broken perennial grassland. Spring barley: pulses, sugar beet, grain maize, soybeans.

How is granular fertilizer applied in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are spread with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader, and the field is then harrowed or disced.

Can organic produce be grown with GREENODIN?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.
