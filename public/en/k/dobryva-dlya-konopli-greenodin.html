GREENODIN fertilizers for hemp

GREENODIN fertilizers for industrial hemp are organo-mineral granular fertilizers that improve the soil's nutrient status. The recommended line is GREENODIN GRAY, with a base rate of 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) before primary tillage. The 3–5 mm granules are applied with a standard spreader, and the 1–2 mm microgranules through the drill's fertilizer hopper.

Fertilizer for hemp. Europe and the USA have regulated the handling of hemp by law, classifying industrial hemp as an ordinary agricultural crop and defining cannabis separately as a crop for strictly medical use. On 2 December 2020, the UN Commission on Narcotic Drugs removed cannabis from Schedule IV of the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs, the category of the most dangerous drugs.

Industrial hemp cultivation is described on the official Diia portal of Ukraine: https://business.diia.gov.ua/idea/silske-gospodarstvo/kultivuvanna-tehnicnih-konopel. GREENODIN hemp fertilizer helps you get a high-quality crop of industrial hemp.

Growing industrial hemp

Hemp's bast-fibre structure contains more than two hundred active substances, 18 of which have been studied. It is used mostly for textiles and oil, with limits on psychoactive compounds such as tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), cannabidiol (CBD) and cannabigerol (CBG). Hemp is widely used in cooking around the world, in pastries and sweets, as well as in medicinal and cosmetic products. It also has uses in construction, fuels and lubricants, and even road building. Hemp fibre is very strong and is used both as tow and as a good raw material for paper.

Because nothing goes to waste, hemp has very high agricultural potential. GREENODIN silica-bearing organic fertilizers enhance its qualities very well. Choosing hemp varieties and hybrids: about 18 hemp varieties are registered in Ukraine, compared with 74 in Europe. The most common include Mykolaichyk, Sofiia, Hlesiia, Hlukhivska-51, Hliana, Sura, Lara and Hloba. When choosing a variety, you can check its registration here: https://regulation.gov.ua/book/165-zelena-kniga-rinok-tehnicnih-konopel

For advice on buying hemp seed, contact our specialists or the Institute of Bast Crops of NAAS directly: http://ibc-naas.com/.

Preceding crops. The best predecessors for hemp are winter cereals, potatoes, sugar beet, maize and vegetables that received high rates of traditional organic fertilizers such as compost or manure, or low rates of professional organic fertilizers like GREENODIN. Pulses and perennial legume grasses are also good predecessors. Sowing hemp after sunflower is a bad idea: volunteer sunflowers infest the field, making harvest harder and lowering product quality, which is especially difficult in natural, organic farming.

Hemp yields well in field crop rotations. It prefers lowland soils and drained peatlands. Other soils can be adjusted with GREENODIN: the different types of fertilizer have their own pH and compensate for the required alkalinity or acidity.

Tillage system

Primary tillage depends on the preceding crop. After stubble crops such as winter cereals or pulses, the stubble is disced in two passes: first to 6 cm, then to 10 cm. Where weeds are heavy, conventional agronomy relies on herbicides, which are not allowed in organic farming, so the field is ploughed two weeks after discing. If hemp follows late crops such as potatoes or sugar beet, the field is ploughed right after the previous crop is harvested. Ploughing depth is 22–25 cm.

Unfortunately, even shallow ploughing harms beneficial soil microorganisms. An effective way to restore them is to apply GREENODIN organic fertilizers containing bacteria that develop in the root zone and produce carboxylic acids, amino acids, polypeptides, polysaccharides, enzymes, phytohormones and natural antibiotics, creating favourable conditions for plant nutrition and development. In spring, once the soil is workable, it is levelled and loosened.

Cultivation combined with levelling and rolling gives a better yield than a single pre-sowing cultivation. On floodplain soils and drained peatlands, autumn ploughing can be replaced by spring discing to 10 cm with simultaneous harrowing and rolling. Applying GREENODIN at this stage at the base rate of 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) effectively improves hemp yield and quality.

Fertilizer use

Hemp responds well to improving the soil's nutrient status with fertilizers. Organic fertilizer rates depend on local soil and climate conditions. In dry regions, applying more than 40 t/ha of manure is ineffective. Elsewhere the average manure rate is 20–40 t/ha, 10–20 t/ha on lowland soils and chernozems, and 3–5 t/ha on peat-bog soils after the first year of cultivation. Such high rates mean corresponding fuel costs and special machinery. GREENODIN organic granular fertilizers can significantly increase hemp yield and quality.

The value of GREENODIN silica-bearing organic fertilizers for field crops, including hemp, lies in higher yield and quality at a rate of just 250 kg/ha, and in their convenient form: granules and microgranules. GREENODIN 3–5 mm granules spread well with a standard fertilizer spreader, and 1–2 mm microgranules through the drill's fertilizer hopper. Applying these fertilizers in hemp production improves the plants' resilience to stress, raises fibre quality and resistance to pathogens, and helps make better use of soil moisture.

Thanks to their organic base of leonardite enriched with polysilicic acids and beneficial microorganisms, these fertilizers restore and increase soil fertility. The natural mineral component of GREENODIN, diatomite made of mobile silicon, serves as a building material for soil bacteria and the plants themselves and as a sorbent for the soil, binding residues of unused synthetic mineral fertilizers, including heavy metals.

In conventional agronomy with mineral fertilizers, the rate on grey and dark grey podzolised soils is N60P90K90–120, and on fertile chernozems N30P45–60K45–60. Phosphorus and potassium fertilizers are recommended in autumn before ploughing, and nitrogen in spring before pre-sowing cultivation. Nitrogen top-dressing is N30–45 per hectare, or 5–6 t/ha of liquid manure or 0.7–1 t/ha of poultry manure.

An effective way to enhance mineral fertilizers, or to replace them partly or fully in top-dressing, is to use GREENODIN organic fertilizers, which are balanced in macro- and micronutrients and contain bacteria that produce these elements. Seed preparation and sowing. Hemp is sown with large-fraction seed of at least 96% purity and at least 75% germination. The higher the germination, the better the yield and fibre quality.

Applying GREENODIN at sowing ensures the best germination thanks to beneficial microorganisms that start developing as soon as they reach soil with some moisture, even from dew.

The sowing method depends on the end use: seed, green stems or dual purpose. Ordinary row sowing with 15 cm row spacing is used for green-stem and dual-purpose hemp. Seed crops are grown in wide rows (45 cm) or bands. The seeding rate is 80–90 kg/ha for monoecious and 100–115 kg/ha for dioecious hemp.

Hemp is sown together with or right after early spring crops, when the soil at seeding depth warms up to +8…+10 °C. Seeding depth is 2–3 cm on heavy soils with adequate moisture and 3–5 cm on light soils. The mobile silicon in GREENODIN is used by the bacteria that are also part of its composition. It forms a so-called silicon matrix that further protects the seed from stress and supports vigorous development.

Crop care includes breaking up the soil crust by harrowing on the 4th day after sowing. In wide-row crops, inter-rows are cultivated mechanically to control weeds: first to 5–6 cm, then 7–8 cm, then 5 cm. Deep cultivation (12–14 cm) dries out the soil and damages hemp roots. GREENODIN noticeably loosens the soil thanks to its mobile silicon (diatomite), which has a bulk density of 350–400 g per litre.

Conventional crop care relies on herbicides for weeds and insecticides for hemp flea beetle and stem borer, and it is worth noting that diatomite acts as a natural insecticide. Harvesting

When hemp is grown for green stems (fibre), harvesting starts when 46–60% of the seed on the plants is ripe. For dual-purpose crops, the male plants are harvested first and the female plants 35–40 days later. The best method is direct combining, with the crop desiccated 5–6 days before harvest. GREENODIN organic fertilizers actually make hemp fibres stronger and therefore of higher quality.

Which organic fertilizer is best for hemp?

GREENODIN GRAY is recommended for hemp.

What is the fertilizer application rate for hemp per hectare?

The main application is 250 kg/ha (25 g/m²) before primary tillage.

When should fertilizer be applied to hemp?

Before primary tillage or at sowing.

What are the best preceding crops for hemp?

Winter cereals, potatoes, sugar beet, maize, vegetables; pulses and perennial legume grasses. Avoid planting after sunflower.

How is granular fertilizer applied in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are spread with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader, and the field is then harrowed or disced.

Can organic produce be grown with GREENODIN?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.
