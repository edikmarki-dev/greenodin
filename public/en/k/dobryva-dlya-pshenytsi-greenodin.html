Wheat fertilizers

Soil preparation and getting ready to sow winter or spring wheat

Soil preparation for winter or spring wheat depends on many factors, especially the harvest date and the type of preceding crop. It is generally accepted that at least three weeks (21 calendar days) should pass between harvest and sowing wheat again. During this time, the fertile topsoil must be preserved, including its moisture and beneficial microorganisms. To do this, after the previous crop is harvested the soil is fertilized with GREENODIN BLACK or GREENODIN GRAY at 150 kg/ha.

Stubble breakdown can be sped up with the microbial stubble decomposer ECOSTERN at 1.5 L/ha in 150 L/ha of spray solution.

Next, cultivate to a depth of up to 5 cm to reduce moisture loss and destroy weeds. After spring cereals, stubble is usually disced once or twice at 2–3 week intervals: once after annual crops and twice where perennial weeds are developing. Since in organic farming we cultivate instead of discing, a second pass, if needed, goes to the same 5 cm depth. All crop residues become green manure.

Both winter and spring wheat need adequate nutrition. It is guaranteed by the organic fertilizers applied earlier, together with stubble decomposers and decomposition products. Nitrogen-fixing, phosphate-solubilising and potassium-mobilising microorganisms that are already established by sowing time supply the soil with enough nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium.

Conventional wheat growing and why it harms the soil

In conventional, soil-depleting wheat production with a target yield of 5 t/ha, the crop removes 175 kg of nitrogen, 65 kg of phosphorus and 90 kg of potassium per hectare. The optimal acidity on fertile soils is pH 6–7. Split nitrogen application, with a main dose at sowing and further doses according to growth stage, used to be considered the most effective approach.

This task has been solved by preparing the soil and applying balanced GREENODIN fertilizers at sowing at 250 kg/ha.

Fertilization plan for winter cereals in organic farming

The fertilization plan for winter cereals in organic farming is as follows: before sowing, treat the seed with HELPROST® Seeds at 2 L/t in 10 L of water and MIKOFREND® (mycorrhiza) at 1.5 L/t in 10 L of water; at sowing, apply 250 kg/ha of GREENODIN BLACK or GREENODIN GRAY, which contain nitrogen-fixing, phosphate-solubilising and potassium-mobilising microorganisms.

Fertilization plan for spring cereals in organic farming

When growing spring wheat the products are applied at the same stages, but over a shorter period and under different seasonal conditions. It differs sharply in a number of traits: capacity for self-pollination:

  • a weak root system, so nutrients are absorbed poorly
  • sensitivity to low temperatures. Seedlings that emerged at air temperatures of 4 to 7 degrees can withstand a drop to -10 degrees only briefly
  • a tendency to be suppressed by weeds
  • a demand for soil moisture (no lower than 65%)
  • vulnerability to drought
  • An important rule: apply GREENODIN BLACK or GREENODIN GRAY onto the stubble of the preceding crop at 150 kg/ha, boosted with the ECOSTERN microbiological stubble decomposer at 1.5 l/ha for a working solution of 150 l/ha
Savings with GREENODIN fertilizers

The choice of GREENODIN fertilizer depends on the soil type, from slightly acidic to slightly alkaline, on soil properties including organic matter content, and on the crop. The average rate is 150 kg/ha. The product has a cumulative effect, so repeat applications should be reduced by 20%. Starting from the maximum of 250 kg/ha, the rate reaches the minimum of 80 kg/ha by the sixth year.

Supporting products made by BTU-Center are recommended for organic farming and work better together with GREENODIN thanks to its active silicon, which stores beneficial substances.

Which organic fertilizer is best for wheat?

GREENODIN BLACK and GREENODIN GRAY are recommended for wheat. The line is chosen according to soil acidity and composition, and fertilizers can be combined.

What is the fertilizer application rate for wheat per hectare?

The main application is 250 kg/ha at sowing. The minimum rate is 80 kg/ha by year 6 (reduced by 20% each year).

When should fertilizer be applied to wheat?

Before primary tillage or at sowing. After harvest, on stubble or crop residues: 150 kg/ha of BLACK or GRAY on the previous crop's stubble + ECOSTERN 1.5 L/ha.

What soil pH is optimal for wheat?

The optimal soil reaction is pH 6–7.

What are the best preceding crops for wheat?

No specific predecessors are given; allow at least 21 days between harvesting the previous crop and sowing.

How is granular fertilizer applied in the field?

The 3–5 mm granules are spread with a standard mineral fertilizer spreader, and the field is then harrowed or disced.

Is there scientific evidence of effectiveness?

Yes, the fertilizer has been tested by research institutions of NAAS. 2016: GREENODIN soil restorers increased the yield of Zoria Ukrainy wheat: BLACK +25%, GRAY +30% grain. Application with spreaders followed by harrowing, or before discing, is recommended. 2018: yields on the fertilized plots were on average 26.9% (0.63 t/ha) higher than the control. GREENODIN GRAY at 150 or 300 kg/ha before pre-sowing cultivation is recommended: 3.99–4.29 t/ha with 128–140% profitability. The reports are on the Documents tab.

Can organic produce be grown with GREENODIN?

Yes. GREENODIN products have been confirmed by the certification body Organic Standard for use in organic farming in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/848. A scan of the certificate is on the Patents and certificates page.

Why is the application rate reduced every year?

The fertilizer has a cumulative, long-lasting effect: beneficial bacteria and organic matter stay in the soil. That is why the rate is cut by 20% each year, and from the 5th–6th season only the minimum rate of 8 g/m² (80 kg/ha) is applied.
