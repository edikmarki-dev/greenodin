About GREENODIN

GREENODIN is an organo-mineral fertilizer developed in Ukraine by the Avelife Institute of Nanotechnology and Organic Products in Vinnytsia. Its composition is based on natural components, including glauconite and leonardite. No synthetic chemicals.

  • Suitable for different soils and crops
  • A dedicated product for each crop
  • Trial reports from the National Academy of Agrarian Sciences of Ukraine (NAAS): wheat, sunflower, strawberry, garlic, potato, soybean, maize
  • The developments are protected by four Ukrainian patents

Ordinary organic fertilizers and other products of organic origin have little effect on the soil: their main job is to feed the plant. GREENODIN organo-mineral fertilizers increase soil fertility, improve its agronomic properties and boost plant vitality all at once.

Organic matter plays a secondary role in GREENODIN products. The core is a combination of natural silica-bearing minerals and organic raw materials. Together they create an environment for beneficial soil microorganisms that restore and increase soil fertility. Each component reinforces the others, which is why the fertilizers work for a long time.

  • Restoring the topsoil
  • Higher crop yields
  • Less water needed for irrigation
  • Cutting agrochemicals, up to phasing them out completely
  • Soil detoxification
  • Long-lasting, even supply of nutrients to plants
  • Stimulation of plant growth and development
  • Better plant resistance to disease
  • Land reclamation
  • Transition to organic farming within two years without losses for the grower
  • Higher market value of clean, organic produce
